## Initial evidence and questions

The 44-case baseline is fully valid. The largest errors involve dense contact
sequences and amplitude, while high-budget search reaches 805.7234 at 1M. This
suggests investigating local optimization efficiency and controllability as well
as planning. Component correlations alone do not establish a cause.

1. Are geometric curvature limits and guide parameterization limiting attainable
   trajectories? Test coherent shape changes while retaining connected curves.
2. Does local search use measured responses efficiently, particularly where the
   same ten-dimensional geometry has only a few important residual directions?
3. Are arrival priors and learned continuation values allocating search toward
   achievable future quality? Compare alternatives and stronger teachers without
   case identity, seed identity or absolute position in learned features.
4. Can completed-track revision or continuation reuse preserve decisions while
   correcting the highest-value residuals at affordable physical cost?

Initial controls use 12 parent specifications spanning all four strata and a range
of contact densities and target axes. Pilot arithmetic means are screening data;
only the full unchanged weighted suite establishes a candidate headline. A positive
pilot proceeds to full-suite discovery and then canonical confirmation. Promising
ideas can receive revised implementations after an adverse first result.

Large study data: `generated/benchmark-v2/arc-825/`. Record immutable options and
source for each panel. Do not silently replace an experiment's output.

## First allocation milestone (exploratory)

The production baseline reserves at least 1.4 times the nominal full local search
rate for every remaining ride frame before planning. Measured root work is lower,
partly because memoization removes repeated physical evaluations. A 0.7 reserve
factor reaches 799.6641 on all 44 cases at 750k; 44/44 are valid. A 0.4 factor fails
8 of 12 pilot cases. This is an allocation tradeoff, not evidence that construction
can be discarded.

A second source of repeated work crosses search calls: the selected continuation
is searched again after its parent arc is committed. The compiler now prototypes
reuse keyed by the complete ordered physical prefix and evaluation options.
Prefix hashes are lazy; a bounded 32-context cache stores completed measurements
and geometry, never live engine wrappers. Prefix tracking survives detach and
backtracking rebuild. Each reused candidate competes in the current search, and
all simulations and final fixed-engine replays remain charged and checked.

With fixed search breadth, three tests (ordinary, changed start, jitter) produce
identical tracks, reports, proposal counts and viable counts with fewer physical
frames. The combined compiler checks pass 48 tests in 12 files. At the 0.7 reserve,
full-suite exploration reaches **803.1454**, 44/44 valid. Stronger but narrower
continuation probes reach **803.1830**, also 44/44; the apparently larger pilot gain
does not carry through to a meaningful aggregate advantage. Neither result is yet
a canonical confirmation or the 825 target.

First executions of Broyden updates, regression-fitted local response, variable
bend radius, and smooth guide bow did not beat the pilot aggregate. Geometry tests
also compare equal counts of complete response iterations, since adding a control
changes the finite-difference batch size. The adverse results are preserved.
Adaptive local allocation avoids the failures of very aggressive reservation in
its pilot but has not established a full-suite advantage. A separate 6M teacher
study now measures more thoroughly optimized two-interval continuations for model
research; its budget and score will never be represented as a 750k result.

`benchmark/v2/studies/arc-825-research.json`. Detailed records, immutable plans,

## Learned control proposals

A 6M research teacher reaches 814.2046, 44/44 valid, using measured two-interval
continuations with substantially stronger local searches. Its replacement value
model does not improve the 750k pilot. A different use of this data works better:
learn coherent arc-control proposals, then test them with the same physical
search. The scorer and validity checks remain untouched.

The collector reconstructs every teacher prefix and verifies its state against
full-track replay before using it. The 4,056 training rows cover 21 parent
families. Features describe relative body state, incoming motion and the next two
authored intervals; they contain no case identity, seed, absolute position or
absolute frame. Outputs describe the existing connected arc controls, with entry
and exit relative to incoming heading and support relative to interval length.
A modest 32-tree joint regressor supplies candidate controls. These replace some
initial broad proposals; they have no authority to bypass physics or scoring.

The first full-fit model reaches **824.3778** on all 44 cases. Five models trained
with complete parent families held out show useful conditional gains but initially
fail one case at the budget limit, producing a 775.7660 aggregate (43/44 valid).
The failure is not omitted. Root search now reduces its local allocation when
measured construction cost exceeds the capacity remaining in the track, and
normalizes the cost observation to avoid mistaking a reduced search for a cheap
full search. This recovers the failed case at 873.9641. The complete held-family
retest reaches **821.7522**, 44/44 valid; the full-fit compiler reaches **824.4253**,
44/44. The held-family result combines five models and is research evidence.

The shipped model representation omits unused internal-node output vectors.
Independent Python prediction fixtures verify unchanged inference. Relative-body
features pass translation-invariance checks. Adaptive local allocation is enabled
with planning; its unnecessary application to basic low-budget construction was
caught by an existing regression test and removed.

Allocation, proposal-count and local-response trials remain in the evidence.
Further model studies compare training losses in raw control coordinates,
search-step units and training-only variance units. Finalists are evaluated on
the full weighted suite; a pilot arithmetic mean cannot establish a headline.
The 825 goal is still active and has not been claimed complete.

## Finalist selection

The full weighted comparison reverses some pilot rankings. Search-step-weighted
control training reaches 826.3022 (44/44), while expanding the original joint
proposal ensemble from 8 to 12 reaches **828.1228** (44/44; maximum 749,956 actual
frames). The more aggressive 0.6 planning reserve reaches 824.9081. The 12-proposal
candidate is selected for canonical confirmation. Its proposal count scales with
the guide-search allowance; the low-budget basic constructor is preserved.

The model is the original raw-coordinate joint regressor, compacted without
changing any decision or leaf value. The frozen arrival-value model remains the
existing accepted artifact. No extra geometry controls or failed response solvers
are added to the production compiler. Normal connected arcs remain unchanged as
the physical primitive.

Final inference review verified the training library's float32 input conversion.
The portable evaluator now performs the same conversion before tree traversal;
a threshold-boundary test distinguishes it from float64 comparisons. Canonical
confirmation evaluates this corrected implementation, rather than assuming that
ordinary prediction fixtures prove behavior at every decision boundary.

## Final confirmation and interpretation

The corrected, frozen compiler confirms **828.1228** canonically. Every canonical
track, report, score and frame count matches the selected full-suite research
panel. Source, suite, scorer, implementation and judge hashes are bound in the
[checked validation](../benchmark/v2/studies/arc-825-validation.json). Protected
source paths match the original 777 compiler. No score or geometry convention was
changed to obtain the result.

The main improvement is better joint control proposals within the existing arc
search. The teacher's optimized controls are more useful as starting candidates
than as a replacement continuation-value model in this implementation. Exact
evaluation reuse and measured allocation make room to explore these proposals
while finishing the track within the physical allowance. The adaptive reserve
and local allocation solve observed budget failures; they are supported by the
preserved failures and subsequent full-panel reproductions.

The deployed model was trained on development-family teacher trajectories.
Parent exclusion is an additional check against simple family memorization:
each held-family track uses a model whose training parents exclude that family.
Its 825.7756 combines five models and is not a canonical or independent external
holdout score. The qualification panel was also reused after diagnosis. The
headline governs promotion; individual case regressions remain in the evidence.

The actual geometry audit covers all 44 distinct canonical tracks: 4,100 interval
groups, of which 697 use one curve and 3,403 use two. All 299,792 constituent
segments are normal type 0 and form connected substantial curves; the shortest
connected chain measures 15.8052 world units. Those segment counts describe arc
tessellation, not isolated point controls. The primitive implementation itself
is unchanged. All three full vertical production videos use the same compiler;
see [the video review](arc-825-video-review.md).

Higher budget remains an opportunity: 1M improves the aggregate only modestly
over 750k. The unsuccessful geometry, response-solver and alternative-model
executions remain useful evidence rather than proofs those approaches cannot
work. Three inherited 150k failures, 251 existing TypeScript diagnostics, and
the earlier audit's legacy fallback budget issue remain explicitly outstanding.
The production renders pass track contracts but still miss their 2% standing-time
creative floor. Motion quality and broader geometric variety therefore remain
useful future work even after this performance target is achieved.
