# Arc 825 campaign

The owner requests an ambitious, empirical campaign to reach at least 825 on the
fixed canonical 750k V2 headline with coherent normal-line arcs. Baseline 777.8193,
source `291384e2`, branch `codex/arc-825`. Prior accepted branches and archives stay
intact. No benchmark, scorer, detector, target or physics changes.

## Initial evidence and questions

The 44-case baseline is fully valid. The largest errors involve dense contact
sequences and amplitude, while high-budget search reaches 805.7234 at 1M. This
suggests investigating local optimization efficiency and controllability as well
as planning. Component correlations alone do not establish a cause.

1. Are geometric curvature limits and guide parameterization limiting attainable
   trajectories? Test coherent shape changes while retaining connected curves.
2. Does local search use measured responses efficiently, particularly where the
   same ten-dimensional geometry has only a few important residual directions?
3. Are arrival priors and learned continuation values allocating search toward
   achievable future quality? Compare alternatives and stronger teachers without
   case identity, seed identity or absolute position in learned features.
4. Can completed-track revision or continuation reuse preserve decisions while
   correcting the highest-value residuals at affordable physical cost?

Initial controls use 12 parent specifications spanning all four strata and a range
of contact densities and target axes. Pilot arithmetic means are screening data;
only the full unchanged weighted suite establishes a candidate headline. A positive
pilot proceeds to full-suite discovery and then canonical confirmation. Promising
ideas can receive revised implementations after an adverse first result.

Large study data: `generated/benchmark-v2/arc-825/`. Record immutable options and
source for each panel. Do not silently replace an experiment's output.

## First allocation milestone (exploratory)

The production baseline reserves at least 1.4 times the nominal full local search
rate for every remaining ride frame before planning. Measured root work is lower,
partly because memoization removes repeated physical evaluations. A 0.7 reserve
factor reaches 799.6641 on all 44 cases at 750k; 44/44 are valid. A 0.4 factor fails
8 of 12 pilot cases. This is an allocation tradeoff, not evidence that construction
can be discarded.

A second source of repeated work crosses search calls: the selected continuation
is searched again after its parent arc is committed. The compiler now prototypes
reuse keyed by the complete ordered physical prefix and evaluation options.
Prefix hashes are lazy; a bounded 32-context cache stores completed measurements
and geometry, never live engine wrappers. Prefix tracking survives detach and
backtracking rebuild. Each reused candidate competes in the current search, and
all simulations and final fixed-engine replays remain charged and checked.

With fixed search breadth, three tests (ordinary, changed start, jitter) produce
identical tracks, reports, proposal counts and viable counts with fewer physical
frames. The combined compiler checks pass 48 tests in 12 files. At the 0.7 reserve,
full-suite exploration reaches **803.1454**, 44/44 valid. Stronger but narrower
continuation probes reach **803.1830**, also 44/44; the apparently larger pilot gain
does not carry through to a meaningful aggregate advantage. Neither result is yet
a canonical confirmation or the 825 target.

First executions of Broyden updates, regression-fitted local response, variable
bend radius, and smooth guide bow did not beat the pilot aggregate. Geometry tests
also compare equal counts of complete response iterations, since adding a control
changes the finite-difference batch size. The adverse results are preserved.
Adaptive local allocation avoids the failures of very aggressive reservation in
its pilot but has not established a full-suite advantage. A separate 6M teacher
study now measures more thoroughly optimized two-interval continuations for model
research; its budget and score will never be represented as a 750k result.

Reproducible checked panel summaries are generated by
`scripts/benchmark/summarize_arc825.py` into
`benchmark/v2/studies/arc-825-research.json`. Detailed records, immutable plans,
source snapshots and failed trials remain in the local study directory.
