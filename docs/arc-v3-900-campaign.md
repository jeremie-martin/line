## Initial research

The baseline evidence attributes substantial remaining loss to endings. Code
analysis finds that the final search interval measures through the half-second
survival grace, whereas the authored ending finishes at the specification
duration. The compiler also adds interval errors with equal weight, although
V3 weights span errors by elapsed time and impact errors by contact count.

First isolate authored-horizon measurement and time-aware planning. These are
compiler objective changes, not benchmark changes. Compare separately before
combining, preserve adverse outcomes, then examine remaining error and search
allocation. Future work may change geometry or learn better proposals when the
evidence supports it; the first hypotheses do not restrict the campaign.
