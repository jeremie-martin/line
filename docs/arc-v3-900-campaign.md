# Arc V3 900 campaign

Target: at least **900** on frozen V3, 88 specifications × seeds 16/17, within
750k actual compiler physics frames. Baseline: **853.9779**, all 176 runs valid.
The established substantial normal-arc style remains required. No judge changes.

## Initial research

The baseline evidence attributes substantial remaining loss to endings. Code
analysis finds that the final search interval measures through the half-second
survival grace, whereas the authored ending finishes at the specification
duration. The compiler also adds interval errors with equal weight, although
V3 weights span errors by elapsed time and impact errors by contact count.

First isolate authored-horizon measurement and time-aware planning. These are
compiler objective changes, not benchmark changes. Compare separately before
combining, preserve adverse outcomes, then examine remaining error and search
allocation. Future work may change geometry or learn better proposals when the
evidence supports it; the first hypotheses do not restrict the campaign.

Code and compact evidence are pushed. Raw studies and full tracks stay local
under `generated/benchmark-v3/arc-900/`. Historical V2 and the published initial
V3 baseline remain unchanged.

## First measured results

A 16-case pilot covers every original non-music family, Believer, a new long
supported program, and another production reference. Its unchanged control
reproduces all 16 published baseline track hashes exactly.

Measuring the authored ending improves all 16 pilot cases. On the complete
88-case seed-16 panel it reaches **873.7992**, with 88 valid runs, 86 improving
and two regressing. This is research evidence, not the final two-seed public
compiler confirmation. The change preserves physical validation through the
existing half-second survival grace.

Equal-to-time objective weighting alone has mixed results: nine pilot cases
improve and seven regress, arithmetic mean delta +0.78185. Combining it directly
with the ending fix underperforms the ending fix alone. One cause to investigate
is transferring already-weighted response matrices between differently weighted
intervals; another is the learned future-value model's old cost scale. These
remain separate hypotheses, not accepted explanations of every regression.

The public amplitude measurement clamps at one. Using a continuous internal
overflow penalty gives the search direction when a curve is too large. Raw and
logarithmic versions improve Open Hook from 732.36 after the ending fix to
770.21 and 768.61, respectively. Both pilot means rise about 2.3 further points;
one other amplitude case regresses under the raw version. The benchmark's
amplitude ruler and all reported measurements remain unchanged.

The first study harness compared repository-wide Git status as part of its
final compiler-identity check. Adding tests and analysis scripts triggered that
metadata assertion after all cells completed. The retained cells were finalized
only after exact verification of compiler bytes, compiler diff, HEAD, engine,
environment, judge, runner, plans, track hashes and budgets. No simulation or
result was replaced. `arc_v3_finalize.ts` records this recovery explicitly;
subsequent panels bind executable identity without unrelated Git-status metadata.

Fourteen focused tests pass, including authored-ending versus survival-grace
measurement and rescaling stored responses through physical residual units.
Further studies compare air-boundary prediction, response-model rescaling,
future-value removal, and allocation of more work to measured local correction.

## Preserved 885 research compiler

The ending correction plus raw internal amplitude overflow reaches **877.5730**
on all 88 cases. Replacing the measured examples with 7,981 replay-verified V3
controls, while retaining the existing forest, reaches **884.2863**. These are
explicit development-trained proposals: their runtime features contain relative
physical state and upcoming targets, without source, seed, index or absolute
position identifiers. Every proposed curve is physically evaluated.

A short-probe allocation defect was identified: a 32-sample lookahead has only
15 proposal slots after its analytic center, while the requested mixture could
contain 20 or more proposals. Concatenation dropped later proposal types. The
compiler now apportions the requested mixture to available slots, including
resizing the forest/example mixture itself. This reaches **885.0206** and is
confirmed through the public compiler on **176/176 canonical runs**, all 88
specifications and seeds 16/17. Every output matches its research track and
physics-frame count. Total compiler work is 130,460,832 frames; the maximum is
749,994. All material is normal, with no isolated single-segment component.
The compiler is preserved at `77e5fba8` on `archive/arc-v3-885`; the 900 target
remains active. [Canonical evidence](../benchmark/v3/studies/arc-885-validation.json)

The full suite rejected time weighting with rescaled memory (**867.6155**) and
the initial larger response allocation (**862.1137**). A second teacher-example
iteration reaches **877.9917**, below the selected first iteration. Smaller
response perturbations, cheaper/deeper lookahead, and one-frame air projection
also fail to establish an improvement. All favorable and adverse complete
studies are indexed in [compact evidence](../benchmark/v3/studies/arc-900-research.json).

Separating the overflow search surrogate from bounded candidate selection is
implemented experimentally. It does not improve the selected compiler's pilot;
a combination with more response work is promising in the pilot but has not
yet passed a full-suite comparison. It remains disabled in the preserved compiler.

Next structural research separates late arc easing from impact-section easing.
Both currently share one shape parameter. Geometry remains substantial connected
normal curves, with the same curvature limit and physical validation.

The first independent-easing executions regress. Further diagnosis found two
implementation/allocation concerns: inherited parameter values must be explicit
when taking independent finite differences, and incomplete response rounds had
reserved work that they could not use. Corrected executions and a version that
adds the parameter only during refinement of longer supports remain exploratory.
The corresponding controls and regressions are retained. A separate 3M-frame
teacher runs in the immutable 885 checkout to supply research trajectories;
its results cannot satisfy the 750k goal. A missing data link in that checkout
was corrected before any teacher compilation began.
