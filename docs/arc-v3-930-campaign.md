# Arc V3 above-930 campaign

Active target: **strictly above 930** on the frozen complete V3 benchmark,
starting from **922.6418**, compiler `93201349`, preserved on
`archive/arc-v3-922`. The owner confirms continued alignment with the coherent
normal-arc style. The same 750,000 actual compiler physics frames apply to each
of the 88 specifications × seeds 16/17. No scorer, target, detector or physics
changes are allowed.

Initial work audits the remaining physical losses and allocation of search work.
The preceding campaign established that verified deeper-search controls can
improve the fixed-budget compiler through residual proposal learning. This
campaign will test that transfer at stronger visited states while also inspecting
whether planning and refinement use their allowance effectively. Offline fit
alone is not a live compiler improvement.

Complete research panels include every case and invalid-run zero; partial
studies have no headline. Preserve favorable and adverse evidence. Final
confirmation uses the public canonical entry point and separate target-jitter
diagnostics. Large datasets, model variants and raw tracks stay local under
`generated/benchmark-v3/arc-930/` and `generated/benchmark-v3/arc-930-teachers/`.

## Initial diagnosis and studies

The baseline audit independently reconstructs each canonical squared-axis loss
and verifies exact research/canonical track, score and frame parity. Endings
account for 8.23% of case-pooled squared loss. Planning spends 25,370,603 of
65,108,264 frames, changes 4,654 local choices across 24,989 probes, and uses
depth two at only 45 of 7,982 decisions. These are diagnostics, not alternative
headline formulas.

All initial panels cover the complete 88-case seed-16 suite at 750k, all valid:

| Change from 922 | Headline |
| --- | ---: |
| Continuation guidance 12 → 24 samples | 915.0322 |
| Two response secant updates between full measurements | 920.7108 |
| 96 base-search / 160 joint-guidance samples | 923.7055 |
| 64 base-search / 192 joint-guidance samples | 910.3157 |
| Residual strength 1.15 → 1.30 | 921.2944 |

The 96/160 allocation preserves the 256-sample nominal total where the original
search is fully provisioned, while moving work toward the joint geometry search.
These research overrides also raise smaller duration-dependent allocations;
production integration must explicitly account for that difference. Its gain does not justify assuming
that more of that shift is always better: the 64/192 version regresses sharply.

The 922 compiler with 3M research frames reaches **931.6134**, all 88 valid.
Maximum actual work is 2,897,108 frames; 7,626 of 7,981 planning decisions use
depth two. Its independently replay-checked prefixes provide 7,981 catch-control
examples. This higher-budget headline is not a standard-budget candidate.
New forest, residual and continuation models are tested in live compilation.
Nested residual Python and TypeScript predictions agree within 2.23e-16 in
encoded control units. Another teacher queries alternatives at fixed 922 states.

Two separate proposal studies test action diversity across forest leaves and
retrieval using supervised tree neighborhoods. All proposed controls still build
the same normal curves and undergo ordinary physical simulation. A stale tracked
control-model checksum sidecar was corrected; the prior canonical evidence had
already hashed and verified the actual model bytes, so its result is unchanged.

## Transfer and solver checkpoint

The best complete 750k research result at this checkpoint is **925.6364**, all
88 cases valid. This is below the goal and has not replaced the public compiler.
The compact research ledger retains every completed panel and paired case
changes, including regressions. Distinct prototype patches are preserved beside
it; raw experiments and alternative model bytes stay local.

Control-supervised retrieval ranks measured examples by shared forest leaves,
using ordinary physical-feature distance to break ties. It reaches 923.3247
alone and **925.5839** with the 96/160 allocation. Runtime inputs contain physical
state and authored targets, with no specification or seed identity. Forest-leaf
diversification, direct teacher-example replacement, several residual transfers,
and learned local corrections did not improve this result. Their offline parity
checks establish correct inference, not useful live decisions.

Actual-displacement finite differences and a box-constrained response solve pass
independent numerical checks. Their original three-unit trust region regresses
in all three ablations. Combining both with a one-unit region reaches 925.6364;
the small difference from retrieval alone is not grounds for claiming a solver
breakthrough. The bounds audit counts committed controls near limits, not actual
clipped probes.

The original compiler reaches 933.1140 with 6M research frames and deeper search.
The retrieval/allocation combination reaches **935.1842 with 3M**, all 88 valid.
Those higher-budget trajectories provide independently replay-checked training
examples for another transfer. A training-only best-trajectory envelope from
earlier teachers yields a live 925.2403; the envelope itself is not a deployable
compiler score. New continuation-value models also regress. Their labels include
the continuation search's terminal heuristic, so they are not pure future loss.

The active next studies test the 935 teacher's supervised neighborhoods and a
larger measured-example proposal share. The frozen public 922 result remains
the promotion baseline until canonical validation succeeds.

## Candidate above 930

The strongest completed research panel reaches **930.1556**, all 88 cases valid
at 750k. The selected changes are supervised retrieval of 7,981 independently
replayed controls from the **935.1842 / 3M** teacher, a **32-proposal quota**
split 1:3 between the existing learned component and measured examples, and
96 base / 160 joint-guidance samples with 138 response samples. The mean
predictor and continuation-value model are unchanged from 922.

The quota interaction is substantial and teacher-dependent:

| Measured examples and retrieval | Quota / weights | Headline |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 931 teacher, supervised retrieval | 16 / 1:1 | 926.2321 |
| 931 teacher, supervised retrieval | 24 / 1:2 | 926.0547 |

| 935 teacher, supervised retrieval | 24 / 1:2 | 929.6826 |

Exploring a second response basin reaches 925.8192 with the earlier retrieval
configuration. Combining bounded response with the stronger 24-proposal setup
reaches 928.3757. Adjusting examples by the supervised predictor's input-to-input
control difference reaches 928.7069; this revisits an earlier transfer idea with
new neighborhoods and data, without improving the unadjusted candidate.
These mechanisms remain research prototypes. A cheaper, narrower continuation
study was already running when the target was crossed; it finishes at 926.9354,
all 88 valid, and remains unselected.

The public allocation now caps initial/local curve work at 96 and grows joint
guidance to 160 from available work per ride frame. Its nominal guide rate is
2.25 times spare allowance beyond the initial breadth threshold; response work
receives 138/160 of that guide allocation. The existing planning and memory
calibration remains separate. The 32 learned/measured proposal slots scale with
guide capacity. This duration-and-budget formula exactly matches the selected
research settings on all 88 V3 specifications; actual work remains governed by
adaptive construction and the hard physics meter. It also changes allocation
at smaller budgets, which is covered by physical compiler tests.

Independent Python retrieval fixtures match TypeScript exactly for 24 query
states at both 4- and 24-example quotas. Normal connected-arc geometry is unchanged.
The integrated public compiler is entering canonical and jitter validation;
the goal is not complete until those checks and preservation finish.
