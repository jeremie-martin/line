## Initial diagnosis and studies

The baseline audit independently reconstructs each canonical squared-axis loss
and verifies exact research/canonical track, score and frame parity. Endings
account for 8.23% of case-pooled squared loss. Planning spends 25,370,603 of
65,108,264 frames, changes 4,654 local choices across 24,989 probes, and uses
depth two at only 45 of 7,982 decisions. These are diagnostics, not alternative
headline formulas.

All initial panels cover the complete 88-case seed-16 suite at 750k, all valid:

| Change from 922 | Headline |
| --- | ---: |
| Continuation guidance 12 → 24 samples | 915.0322 |
| Two response secant updates between full measurements | 920.7108 |

The 96/160 allocation preserves the 256-sample nominal total where the original
search is fully provisioned, while moving work toward the joint geometry search.
These research overrides also raise smaller duration-dependent allocations;
production integration must explicitly account for that difference. Its gain does not justify assuming
that more of that shift is always better: the 64/192 version regresses sharply.

## Transfer and solver checkpoint

The best complete 750k research result at this checkpoint is **925.6364**, all
88 cases valid. This is below the goal and has not replaced the public compiler.
The compact research ledger retains every completed panel and paired case
changes, including regressions. Distinct prototype patches are preserved beside
it; raw experiments and alternative model bytes stay local.

Control-supervised retrieval ranks measured examples by shared forest leaves,
using ordinary physical-feature distance to break ties. It reaches 923.3247
alone and **925.5839** with the 96/160 allocation. Runtime inputs contain physical
state and authored targets, with no specification or seed identity. Forest-leaf
diversification, direct teacher-example replacement, several residual transfers,
and learned local corrections did not improve this result. Their offline parity
checks establish correct inference, not useful live decisions.

Actual-displacement finite differences and a box-constrained response solve pass
independent numerical checks. Their original three-unit trust region regresses
in all three ablations. Combining both with a one-unit region reaches 925.6364;
the small difference from retrieval alone is not grounds for claiming a solver
breakthrough. The bounds audit counts committed controls near limits, not actual
clipped probes.

The original compiler reaches 933.1140 with 6M research frames and deeper search.
The retrieval/allocation combination reaches **935.1842 with 3M**, all 88 valid.
Those higher-budget trajectories provide independently replay-checked training
examples for another transfer. A training-only best-trajectory envelope from
earlier teachers yields a live 925.2403; the envelope itself is not a deployable
compiler score. New continuation-value models also regress. Their labels include
the continuation search's terminal heuristic, so they are not pure future loss.

The active next studies test the 935 teacher's supervised neighborhoods and a
larger measured-example proposal share. The frozen public 922 result remains
the promotion baseline until canonical validation succeeds.
