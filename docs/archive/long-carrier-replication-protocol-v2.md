# Retired Long-Carrier Replication V2

Status: superseded before any formal declaration, fixture, assay, or ledger was
published.

V2 corrected V1's exact-callback observation defect with a physical-prefix
projection rule. During pre-execution mechanical review, its
`open_high_air_195` control was found to have no viable opening contact at the
fixed WASM/500k budget. That is a roster-design failure, not a negative assay
result.

V3 replaces that source with a manual ordinary-opening, high-air-reentry
control and adds a sealed all-row mechanical feasibility qualification before a
cohort declaration. See
[`docs/long-carrier-replication-protocol.md`](../long-carrier-replication-protocol.md).
