## Standing regime

Inherited whole from the closed plan — the gold standard as evidence with the
campaign owning the decision; promote unless the negative boundary or an
anomaly, investigate before judging; deliberate `--force` promotions with the
numbers recorded; bundles as candidates; stale-sweep ledger; comment hygiene;
one deadline signal; the observation invariant — plus the two lessons that
program paid for:

- **Action-set power.** No preview is believed until the candidate's action
  set is counted: how many cells CAN it change, and what failure rate would
  the observed zero exclude? (1b's clean preview had P(zero collapses) ≈ 0.55
  on 14 changed cells.) The 7-source mini-manifest instrument (4-minute
  48-seed mover grids, verified compile-identical) is the standard tool; Track
  T promotes it into the repo.
- **Composition re-probes.** Sequential candidates in one mechanism family are
  re-probed against the current tree before their eval. The tree between
  candidates is also a composition.

Both were used in anger and both earned their keep: the power footer is what
made Phase 3's zero-loss grid *evidence* (an observed zero on a 243-cell action

hope, and the L1 composition re-probe against the post-Phase-3 tree is what
established the lane was still open (51.3% of verdicts false, nothing collapsed)

## Phase 1 — revisitable depth — **CLOSED AS MEASURED 2026-08-03**

**The premise is falsified, and the mechanism worked.** Both halves of that
sentence are the finding. Evidence: `scratchpad/dividends-p1/` (eight arms on a
collapse-enriched 7-source manifest × 48 seeds × 750k, 336 cells, 0 invalid in
control), reproduced in map §6.1.

**The dividend does not exist.** Shallowing hop 2 across the whole
capability-mover population frees **878 frames of 765,198 — 0.115% of the
compile** (0.065% over all 336 cells; 0.115% is the most favourable
denominator). The reason is structural: **51.0% of rollout calls dead-end at hop
1**, where depth 1 and depth 2 are the same computation, so half the lever is a
no-op by construction. Phase 4 later ceilinged the whole family independently —
hop-2 frames are 2.5% / 9.0% / 2.5% of the compile at 150k / 250k / 750k, and
removing hop 2 outright costs −3.84 ± 3.35 per cell at 750k.

**Everything 1b appeared to do was a width effect.** Splitting the mechanism
into `headdepth` (the shallow-rolled tail keeps its score instead of being
scored `Infinity` — pure selectability, **zero frames freed**) and `shallowhead`
(the head drops its second hop too — pure cost, no width): the width half
reproduces **93% of the healed upside (+13.00 of +14.03) while freeing zero
frames**, and carries the collapse risk with it (5 of 83 changed cells against
the lottery's 4 of 90); the cost half — the actual depth dividend — is **−16.24
healed, 51 cells down against 29 up, and collapses nothing.** On the changed
cells the score tracks `deepest_seen_gap` at r = +0.916 and frames freed at
+0.295. The mechanism was never about frames.

**Revisitability worked and still lost by 16×.** `revisit1` re-deepens exactly
as designed — on the shallowed choice's own placement failure — and **rescued 2
of the lottery's 4 known losses at no upside cost** (healed +14.07 against
+14.03, 2 lost on 90 changed). Pooled across the eight arms the loss rate is
**35 of 702 changed cells = 4.99% [3.47%, 6.93%]** against a break-even under
**0.30%**. Making the lottery reversible bounds the tail; it does not move the
order of magnitude. Priced honestly, a clean run on 90 cells excludes only
rates above 3.33% — **1,000 changed cells are needed to exclude 0.30%**, which
is itself the action-set power rule doing its job before an eval slot was spent.
One debit recorded rather than waved off: revisit1 raised cross-state pool reads
6 → 38 on one pressed compile — no new memo key, but a changed visit order
re-enters pools built under a different aim-lane throttle state.

**Reopeners, named:** (1) the **width lever as its own mechanism family** —
+13.00 healed for zero frames, with the collapse tail still attached and no
reversibility story yet; (2) **share-0.2 rate-bounded depth**, which pooled over
three seed blocks reads 232 changed / 1 lost = 0.43% against 1.407% tolerable
and clears break-even ~3× — but its raw capability sum by block is +103.06 /
−0.34 / +6.61, so it pays only where baseline failures exist to rescue, which
makes it an **operating-point** reopener, not a mechanism one; (3) the **250k
sign flip**, with the completion ledger stated in §0b above. **Closed as
measured: capability debt is not repayable on the depth axis at 750k.**

## Track P — probes — **ALL THREE TERMINAL**

- **Acceptance signal probe: CLOSED, second null, 2026-08-03.** Does the
  margin's trajectory at first completion predict repair acceptance? No. On
  1,501 trace compiles and 2,107 canonical ones, no covariate clears both
  held-out lift and a Holm-corrected p; the five candidates are one variable
  (|r| 0.93–0.98); the best trace-750k lifts (+0.0034 to +0.0045 nats, Holm p
  0.20–0.46) fail to replicate on the 5.4×-larger canonical corpus where every
  lift is *negative*; and all of it is dwarfed by the attempt ordinal at
  0.0148–0.0233 nats/attempt. **The economics is what closes the lane, not the
  fit.** A perfect oracle — flag every zero-yield compile ex ante and redirect
  100% of its repair frames at the observed ordinal≥2 marginal rate of 0.0077
  pts/kf — is worth **+0.45 to +0.48 points per compile**, and every reachable
  policy is negative (skip the worst-predicted 5 / 10 / 20 / 30% → −0.046 /
  −0.136 / −0.354 / −0.616): the enrichment is real (1.7–2×) and useless,
  because the flagged compiles are already the ones spending least. And **the
  stratification is the null model's own mediator** — the 3× spread in
  zero-yield across `firstCompletionFrac` quintiles (10.4% → 31.0%) is 80–85%
  reproduced by the (ordinal × local-budget) coin-flip cell model already on
  record, with the residual tracking attempt *count* (observed/model 1.22 at one
  attempt, 0.59–0.81 at three or more). The margin trajectory tells you how many
  coin flips get tossed, not how they land. Do not re-open on a better feature.
  A data fact worth carrying: `hard_completion_margin` is null on 100% of
  observations at 150k / 300k / 1.5M, so the trajectory corpus is 394 compiles,
  not 1,408.

  against 1.407% tolerable. The bound exists; what it does not do is transfer
  across seed blocks (+103.06 / −0.34 / +6.61 raw capability), which is why it

## Phase 4 — the forward-evaluation smell — **LANDED 2026-08-03 (L1 accepted)**

**The observation, as stated when the phase opened:** 51% of forward-eval
rollout calls end at a hop-1 dead-end verdict, on a mechanism that is ~half of
every compile's charged frames. The owner's fork, verbatim in spirit: *if this
is real signal it is amazing; if it is artifact it is catastrophic; the answer
probably lies between, and it is a huge smell that must be carefully
understood.* Nothing in this repo had ever verified the verdicts' truth.

**Three of the phase's own premises were wrong, and the study says so before it
says anything else** ([`rollout-economics-study.md`](rollout-economics-study.md),
`525d1de`). *The 51% was the wrong population*: the literal dead-end verdict is
13.6–20.2% of linear rollout calls (6.4–16.4% of all calls); what reproduces at
51% is rollouts that place at most one hop, 48.3% pooled — and only verdicts can
be true or false, so sizing the truth question off the 51% overstates it ~3×.
*Rollouts are not half the frames*: rollout + start-eval frames are 29.1% /
27.7% / 20.5% at 150k / 250k / 750k on the instrumented grid (34.7% / 32.4% /
29.4% across 3,648 archived compiles); the old figure predates the closed-form
ballistic cutover. *And the dead-end rate is a property of the source mix, not
of the compiler*: 10.3% to 78.1% across 44 sources, with the six above 60% all
capability-frontier members.

**The finding, at 100% sampling of 31,494 verdicts.** The same generator, at the
same node, through the same gates, finds a viable catch for **16,791 of them —
53.3% of the verdicts are false**; 14,703 (46.7%) are verified true. Both numbers
are bounds in the same direction: 53.3% is a **lower bound on the error rate**
and 46.7% an **upper bound on correctness**, because the ladder reproduces only
the ordinary sampling lane and never draws the reuse lane, the brake lane,
kinematic support, or the startup rescue tier's distinct catch stream — every one
of which can only add candidates. "Verified true" means "still dead after
everything the *ordinary* generator can do". **Correction to
the campaign's own record:** the study's §4.2 pooled row transposes its TRUE and
FALSE columns and the section heading, the lane table and two commit messages
inherited it as "46.7% false". The per-budget rows sum to 14,703 true, the
refutation ladder's own "never (verdict true)" row reads 14,703, and the stratum
table reweights to 46.7% true. Direction of the error: **the verdicts are worse
than claimed, not better** — no conclusion flips, and the shipped docstring's
51.3% (the post-Phase-3 re-probe) is internally consistent and correct. Filed as
map discrepancy 17.

*The cause is breadth and it is a factor of 24–81*: the rollout's hop-1
expansion is one `sampleOneCandidate` attempt at index 0, where the search at
the same node draws 23.5 / 30.3 / 80.9 and then adds the aim lane, two extra
lanes and three rescue tiers. "The dead-end verdict is not *no continuation
exists here*; it is *draw number 0 did not catch*." *The error splits by
terrain, not by pool rank*: capability 58.9% verified-true, legacy 40.5%,
representative 30.3%, `high_air_drive` 5.3% — while by pool rank it is flat to
two points (47.2% → 47.3%), which **falsifies branch 3** outright. Against the
realized search (32,733 joined nodes, an independent no-probe ruler) false-alive
is 0.0% and false-dead 21.6%. So the owner's "probably between the two" resolves
into a **boundary, not a blend**: terrain information on the frontier, breadth
artifact elsewhere, applied identically to both.

**Branch 2 is the dominant explanation, and lane 2 is what shipped: L1,
re-draw on empty** (`bb45125`). When a charged rollout's first rolled contact
expands to nothing, draw **one** more sample before the verdict stands — the
rule the search already applies to itself in three rescue tiers, one level up on
the mechanism that never had it. The trigger is the node's own outcome, not a
threshold on a signal, so the campaign's "throttle a magnitude, never trigger a
mode" caveat does not apply; determinism is free under `getCandidatesSorted`'s
per-gap prefix contract (the extra draw *is* the sample the search would take
next); the aim lane is suppressed with save/restore, because §4.5 measured that
it contributes nothing to the refutation and 4.6× to the cost. The dose is
arithmetic rather than taste: at ~40 frames a draw the marginal cost per
marginal correction is 261 / 628 / 845 frames for a ladder to 2 / 3 / 5, so
**draw 2 buys a correction 2.4× cheaper than draw 3**. And the widened pool is
left in the memo **on purpose** — `_candidatesCache` is keyed on `(seed, nCand)`
and the re-draw writes only widths 2 and 4, disjoint by construction from every
other consumer (1, 8, 16, or the search pool whose breadth law floors at 8) — so
`cachedForwardContinuation` reports the corrected bit and **L1 heals L3 at the
source**.

*Ladder.* Composition re-probe against the post-Phase-3 tree first (lane open:
51.3% false, nothing collapsed, the dead-verdict population ~11% larger). Then:
capability movers 48 seeds at 750k **+1.68 ± 0.48 (t = +3.48), 0 lost / 0
gained**, all capability groups positive with controls flat, POWERED at 6× its
break-even; full 44 × 8 at 750k +0.52 ± 0.65, 0/0; 500k over two disjoint seed
blocks pooled +2.27 ± 1.01, net +1 completion; 250k both-valid +1.87 ± 0.76.
The quality effect is **flat across the whole budget profile** (+1.87 / +1.85 /
+1.68 at 250k / 500k / 750k both-valid, t up to +4.24), which is the scale
contract satisfied by measurement rather than by argument. Realized price
**0.45–1.71% of frames**, under prediction because the memo makes a repeat
dead-end at an already-re-drawn node free. Low-budget completion churn priced
honestly as a reshuffle rather than as a win: 58 flips, net +8, z = +1.05, and
the true event cost is ~410–425 points, not the tool's default 50.

**Accepted at the N=32 boundary** — the program's first boundary accept:
P(+) **99.02%** against a required 98.88%, **+0.72 [−0.08, +1.53]**, validity
1408/1408, every stratum positive (representative +0.39, capability +1.39,
legacy +2.27, music +0.27). Headline 595.23 → **595.97**; ledger +0.04.

*Two things stated rather than hidden.* The "can only add candidates" claim is a
strong prior, not a proof: `kinematicEligible` requires
`poolForwardContinuationAbsent`, so refuting a verdict can switch the
kinematic-support rescue *off* at that pool. And one genuine non-lottery
negative survives: `frontier_low_air_endurance` at 250k, **−4.24 ± 1.74** at
48/48 valid in both arms, positive at 500k and 750k — it reads as a low-budget
frame-allocation cost rather than a mechanism fault, and it is the one cell
block worth a look.

*What the phase priced and left.* **The whole "cheaper rollouts" family is
capped at +1.78 ± 1.31** — that is what refunding *every* rollout frame at 750k
is worth, so correctness and not cost is where the value is. Three lanes remain
with prices on them: **L2**, the impact widening, which is 62.8% of rollout
frames at 750k and buys +4.59 ± 2.20 per cell — the untried trade is narrowing
it and spending the frames on the re-draw everywhere, ~20× the coverage per
frame; **L3**, the online-continuation filter, now fed a corrected bit rather
than fixed, with its own firing rate still uncollected; and the **dose ladder**
beyond one draw. Hop-2 verdicts were deliberately out of scope.
