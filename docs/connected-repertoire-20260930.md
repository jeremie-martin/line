## Selection before the reserved intervention

upward/downward folds, movable corners, and preservation of the chosen structure.
The experiments are working-tree development, not forty-four independent random
trials. Full raw results and failures remain local.

Coarse faces alone often remain nearly straight. A folded three-face construction
produces a clearly different stepped silhouette. Downward folds commonly use the
guide to redirect the rider toward the last main face; the middle main face may

## Delivered construction and its controls

The [music review](http://localhost:8767/motion-gallery/music.html) opens on
**folded phrases**. The main rail has an approach followed by three substantial
straight faces: entry, angled middle and exit. Unlike the earlier facets recipe,
this is an explicit step, rather than a lower-resolution version of a curve.
The guide, when used, is built from the same physical path. Every segment is a
normal line. Finished Luna and Amor frames show the angular silhouette at the
ordinary production camera scale; the subsequent owner review above confirms
that the visual difference is welcome.

| Control | What it actually does |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed face count | Sets physical main faces independently of estimated support duration. Development compares 2–4 plain faces, and folds with 3 or 6. The retained fold uses 3, plus the approach. |
| Fold angle | Offsets the middle heading from the mean of entry-face and exit headings. The retained +30° points downward in screen coordinates. This is not a guaranteed 30° change at both corners: the entry and exit headings are also searched. |
| Corner placement | The existing turn fraction and easing parameter distribute support time among the three faces. Each retains at least 20% of support time; the remaining 40% is distributed. Actual lengths also depend on estimated speed. |
| Strength and onset | Blend/place the profile during construction. The retained example uses full strength from the start; reducing strength is not guaranteed to preserve the visible step or validity. |
| Guidance | The ordinary search adjusts clearance, guide endpoints and flare. Unused guide geometry is pruned. A fold is not a promise that a guide will always, or never, be present. |

These are research construction controls, not new music-specification syntax or
an automatic distribution of styles. The implementation shares arc construction,
physical evaluation, continuation and rendering. Production defaults stay fixed.

## What the rider does

The inspection records actual collision events, which rider point touched which
face, their order, and progress projected from the final state of each frame.
It independently matches all saved body positions and collision-ID hashes.
That projection is not an exact collision position within a solver iteration.

On Luna's first selected fold, the rider contacts the entry face at frames
259–260, the angled guide at 262–263, then the lower face at 264–266. It does not
contact the middle main face. On Amor's first fold, the entry contacts span
79–85 and the lower face is contacted at 91 and 93, with no guide remaining.
The rider crosses the step ballistically. Other Amor folds do contact the middle
face before reaching the exit. Both are meaningful physical interactions, but
neither proves continuous riding around every corner. These are folded passages,
not universally continuous slides or a decorative outline.

## The failed entry and a meaningful diagnostic retry

The frozen Tiki all-folded alternative fails at frame **749 / 18.725 s**. Its
first changed support estimate is approximately five frames. All 43 initial
candidate evaluations fail: 42 lose binding and one changes the locked prefix.
It spends only 6,757 frames including preparation and full replays, despite an
allowance of one million. It never reaches local refinement at that entry.

The inherited initial batch is largely filled by proposals trained on ordinary
arcs. A diagnostic retry removes learned/memory proposals from initialization,
leaving 40 general proposals; a second uses 80 general proposals and a larger
local sample allowance, still within the same total ceiling. Both complete the
same fixed +30° geometry and unchanged musical request:

| Post-confirmation diagnosis | Complete | Whole-ride RMS | Simulated frames |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 40 general initial proposals | Yes | 0.068774 | 861,776 |
| 80 general initial proposals | Yes | 0.045670 | 891,865 |

The [entry diagnosis gallery](http://localhost:8767/motion-gallery/?data=/generated/connected-repertoire-20260930/initialization/manifest.json)
compares those actual rides, the original failure and the baseline. This supports
an initialization-coverage problem; it does not prove that removing learned
proposals generally improves search. The second trial also changes later local
search allocation, so its error improvement cannot be attributed solely to
initial breadth. Both remain worse musically than the baseline and the mixed
construction. They are **post-confirmation development on a known failure**, not
replacement confirmation, production promotion or evidence of broad robustness.
The original failed outcome remains in the primary panel.

A concrete next engineering experiment is bounded recovery when no initial
proposal survives, with construction-aware proposal coverage and accounting for
all attempts. Compare learned, general and physically adapted proposals on folds
and the earlier difficult ripple entries. Preserve the visible construction while
measuring validity, musical loss and actual work. Merely increasing the nominal
budget cannot fix an early return before that budget is used. Also inspect
inactive dimensions: at full fold strength, the explicit headings replace the
ordinary bend profile, but shared refinement can still spend probes on it. No
claim of completely geometry-aware or maximally efficient search is made here.
