# A deliberate repertoire for musical tracks

Agreed with the owner on 2026-09-30. This is the active roadmap, following the
[first musical-direction milestone](musical-direction-plan.md).

The product is visually compelling, varied Line Rider tracks synchronized with
music. Accurate compilation is a foundation. We want understandable, reproducible
creative choices, useful control over them, and efficient construction. The owner
found the first faceted phrase too similar to production; the ripple example was
visibly different and meaningful, an encouraging beginning. Neither result
establishes a geometry's potential or a universal artistic preference.

## Roadmap

1. **Understand and adjust existing constructions.** Establish what controls the
   appearance, how the main rail and guide interact, and what search decides.
   Use controlled comparisons to explain and deliberately vary real examples.
   Fix accidental coupling where it obstructs these tasks.
2. **Develop substantially different choices.** Give existing serpentine rails,
   terraces and scattered normal segments thoughtful iterations, working on two
   candidates at a time. Introduce geometry when it supplies something missing.
   Evaluate meaningful differences at finished-video scale, beyond decoration.
3. **Make search effective across choices.** Investigate proposals, parameter
   ranges, allocation and stopping rules for bias toward ordinary arcs. Compare
   actual computational work and local accuracy. Diagnose failures before
   attributing them to the geometry. Preserve promising implementations and
   visual contrast while improving shared machinery.
4. **Compose across musical phrases.** Study entry, sustained use, intensity,
   return and transitions, including guided and unguided possibilities. Start
   with deliberate editorial choices and use their outcomes to determine which
   controls should eventually belong in specifications.
5. **Make the gallery a creative workspace.** Keep faithful native playback and
   finished musical comparisons central. Organize around shape, strength,
   guidance and transitions; disclose musical targets, failures and work. Keep
   worthwhile alternatives available so optimization cannot silently erase the
   visual choice being explored.

These steps inform one another. They do not require a large framework before
practical examples. The first milestone's restriction on extending the geometry
catalog was local to that milestone, not a permanent project constraint.

## Next concrete milestone

Deliver three visibly distinct constructions on the same selected musical
passages, followed by a complete musical comparison combining them intentionally.
Use Luna Bala and Amor na Praia for development, then a further passage reserved
before confirmation to check reuse. Count distinct searches/tracks rather than
treat identical zero-jitter seeds as independent evidence.

Start with ordinary arcs and the encouraging ripple construction. Compare
serpentine and terraces as candidates for the third choice. Investigate their
actual geometry, contact and search behavior; iterate where evidence indicates a
weak implementation. This is a concrete starting point, not a permanent catalog
or a commitment to accept the first candidate.

Deliverables:

- Same-passage comparisons and a concise explanation of each construction's
  adjustable choices, actual contacts and limitations.
- A complete ride with deliberate use of the three constructions, verified
  physical transitions and unchanged authored musical targets.
- Preserved tracks, faithful inspection and full vertical production videos in
  the existing gallery, including the baseline and meaningful alternatives.
- Local musical errors, validity, actual simulated work and compilation time;
  matched-work comparisons for any claimed search improvement.
- Confirmation on a reserved passage after freezing the implementation, plus
  focused regression checks. Shared compiler changes require default-behavior
  checks; benchmark definitions remain frozen.
- A results record that distinguishes technical completion, visual hypotheses,
  owner feedback and remaining work. Owner review is required to establish new
  aesthetic preferences; technical work can proceed independently.

A candidate with poor first results deserves diagnosis and a meaningful retry,
not an automatic rejection. Significant simplification can justify a small
measured accuracy tradeoff; neither regression nor improvement should be hidden
by aggregate scores. No claim of a geometry ceiling follows from this study.

rider bounces past its distinctive bends. Ordered collision inspection confirms
concrete examples; the earlier count of contacts with shaped segments was too
weak to establish traversal. The technical panel is delivered, while a convincing

Before widening this panel, develop one passage where the rider visibly follows
the serpentine's changing direction. Investigate profile placement, curvature,
available contact time and guide interaction with matched incoming states and
unchanged musical targets. Use collision sequences to explain the actual ride,
and finished video to judge the experience. Preserve failed alternatives and
disclose accuracy and work. A bounce can be a deliberate choice elsewhere; it
does not demonstrate the particular riding behavior being explored here. Do not
replace this problem with an automatic contact quota or claim a geometry ceiling.

## Constraints and working practice

Use normal lines only. Do not change benchmark, scorer, detector or physics.
Production preferences remain explicit; no automatic shape quotas or musical
classification are implied. Keep changes small enough to explain, with common
construction and composition paths where the evidence supports them. Avoid
duplicating production or rendering machinery for research.

Commit and push code, documentation and compact reproducible evidence. Keep large
media, raw replays and archives local. The conversation transcript remains local
reference material. Report progress and uncertainty candidly.
