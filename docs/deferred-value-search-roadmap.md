# Deferred-value suffix search roadmap

## Objective

Preserve the useful score-blind signal found by the value-ranked selective-DFS
campaign without delaying or changing the compiler's first complete track.
Initial traversal observes exact branch opportunities but performs no voluntary
work. After the first improving terminal exists, one later experimental arm may
spend bounded work on the best affordable exact alternative before ordinary
repair.

This remains a fixed-budget compiler. The intermediate terminal is protected
because it is the production incumbent and cost profile used to allocate the
rest of that one compile, not because the compiler is becoming anytime.

## Search semantics

1. Initial DFS, candidate generation, ranking, production branch-regret
   catch-up, scoring, and register behavior remain unchanged.
2. At an authored contact boundary, retain a watch only when it first reaches
   the already selected density threshold 0.020 at maturity three or greater.
   Density at collection remains authored-axis regret divided by conservative
   equal-depth probe work. Final score is never read.
3. If the first crossing occurs before 10% normalized authored-gap progress,
   expire only this experimental opportunity. Preserve the underlying watch
   for ordinary DFS and production branch-regret behavior. This carries forward
   the categorical Phase-D result; it is not a new horizon sweep.
4. A retained record owns the exact current prefix object and exact already
   generated causal sibling. No seed change, candidate regeneration, or
   inferred branch is permitted.
5. At the first improving terminal, freeze collection. A record survives only
   when its current prefix is an exact object-identical prefix of that terminal,
   its watch was not consumed by production, and its causal sibling is still
   in the original synchronous frontier.
6. Reprice each survivor at the terminal using conservative measured
   incumbent cost-to-end from the sibling's concrete gap. Rank affordable
   survivors by terminal-time value density, then raw regret, then deeper
   sibling, then stable watch identity.

## Phase A: behavior-neutral survival map

Add an explicit
`LR_FRONTIER_POLICY=selective-axis-regret-catchup-value-deferred-map` mode. It
collects and seals the portfolio but executes no deferred attempt. It records:

- crossing prefix, exact sibling, watch identity, progress, raw regret, and
  collection density;
- early expiration and production-consumed disposition;
- exact first-terminal membership and sibling availability;
- terminal-time hard and repair remainder, conservative full-suffix estimate,
  policy budget, affordability reason, terminal-time density, and stable rank;
- first-terminal frame and track hash.

The fixed map uses the same ten-source compact suite as the preceding campaign,
budgets 750,000 and 1,250,000, and fresh actual seeds 170-173. It contains 80
map cells and 80 production references. Tracks, scores, validity, first-terminal
frame/hash, and repair episode ledgers must be exact in every pair.

The map evaluates exploration shares 15%, 25%, and 40% from the same retained
records and charged-work estimate. This is an offline coverage bracket, not
three compiler arms. Select the smallest share that, at each budget:

- at least 20 exact incumbent-path alternatives are affordable;
- affordable alternatives span at least eight runs and four sources;
- at least eight runs and four sources have a rank-one action; and
- the 1.25M affordable count is at least half the 750k count.

The 1.25M affordable count must also retain at least half the 750k count. If no
share clears, close the action. This is a score-blind coverage gate: final score
does not select the share, and no adjacent share may be substituted later.

## Phase B: one bounded deferred suffix attempt

Only if Phase A clears, add an explicit
`LR_FRONTIER_POLICY=selective-axis-regret-catchup-value-deferred-initial` arm.
It preserves the exact initial traversal and first terminal, then takes the
rank-one sibling from the original frontier and gives it one self-contained
ordinary preferred-frontier suffix search.

- The local allowance is the smaller of the Phase-A-selected share of search
  policy budget, remaining repair budget, and remaining hard budget.
- Admission requires the conservative terminal-time full-suffix estimate to
  fit that allowance.
- Speculative tail completion is disabled inside the optional attempt so one
  penultimate atomic expansion cannot silently consume the rest of the phase.
- Before another atomic node, the same observed candidate-width/atomic-cost
  bound used by the preceding value campaign must fit the remaining local
  allowance. A completed atomic node may overshoot its estimate; no further
  node may start afterward.
- Stop at the first terminal offer, frontier exhaustion, or local allowance.
  Every surviving local frontier node returns to the original frontier; the
  attempt does not prune unfinished alternatives.
- The ordinary repair controller then runs from the best incumbent, which may
  or may not have changed.

Telemetry must distinguish collected, expired, incumbent-path, live,
affordable, selected, terminal reached, register accepted, budget-yielded, and
returned-frontier states. It records requested proposals, geometry evaluations,
atomic frames, actual/estimated work, first-terminal identity, ordinary repair
displacement, and final authored score.

The live screen uses fresh actual seeds 174-177, the same ten sources, and both
budgets. A confirmation is earned only when:

- every candidate cell remains valid;
- the first-terminal frame and track hash are exact in every pair;
- total and active-cell score means are positive at both budgets;
- at least three of four seed blocks are positive at one budget and no fewer
  than two at the other;
- no cell loses 20 points; and
- actions span at least eight runs and four sources at each budget.

A pass earns one fresh confirmation on seeds 178-181 under the exact same
contract. A failure closes the exact rule. No density, horizon, allowance,
candidate breadth, repair policy, or canonical Benchmark V2 run is authorized
inside this campaign.

## Implementation invariants

1. Unset production and its explicit policy remain deterministic and unchanged.
2. Map mode is behavior-neutral, including repair and resumed work.
3. The exact first terminal, not merely its frame, is stable in a live pair.
4. Every selected sibling was generated and queued before the first terminal.
5. Deferred work has one unambiguous post-terminal lane and is never reported
   as an ordinary repair attempt.
6. Budget telemetry and selective telemetry reconcile node work, terminal
   offers, register changes, local ceilings, and returned frontier state.
7. Authored score remains the target; feasibility diagnostics cannot cap or
   rewrite the specification.

## Phase A result

The fixed map completed all 160 compiles with every cell valid. All 80 pairs
are exact in final track, score, first-terminal frame, first-terminal track
hash, and repair episode ledger. The collector therefore has no measured
effect on initial traversal or any post-terminal work.

At 750k, 799 watches cross, 54 expire before the horizon, 745 are collected,
563 lie on the exact first-incumbent path, and 261 are affordable at the map's
40% envelope. Those records provide rank-one actions in 36/40 runs and all ten
sources. At 1.25M, the corresponding counts are 522, 50, 472, 291, and 152;
rank-one actions span 27/40 runs and eight sources.

The score-blind allowance bracket selects 40%. At 15%, coverage is 15 records
in 11 runs/five sources at 750k and 11 records in nine runs/three sources at
1.25M. At 25%, it is 98 records in 20 runs/eight sources and 44 records in 14
runs/six sources; its high-budget count is five records short of the declared
half-retention requirement (44 < 49). At 40%, both budgets clear every clause and
high-budget affordability retains more than half of low-budget affordability.

Phase B is therefore authorized with exactly one attempt, allowance fraction
0.40, and fresh seeds 174-177. No other share, density, horizon, source gate,
or score-derived substitution is permitted.
