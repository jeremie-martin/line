## What changes about the work

The old campaign repeatedly demonstrated local authority without a useful
complete program. Examples include locally improved current/next pairs whose
ordinary suffixes lost 21.41 points on average, and active-material screens
whose gains appeared in tracks containing none of the proposed material.
The searched-pool model also improved an offline proxy but finished its
canonical trial inconclusive-negative. These are distinct failures: absent
physical authority, poor continuation, a misleading proxy, and search-budget
displacement must not be treated as one knob-selection problem.

1. **Price the full objective first.** Use verified accepted archives and exact
   headline aggregation. Record ceilings, assumptions, and source coverage;
   do not call a stitched local oracle a realizable track.
2. **Audit the causal chain before proposing a fix.** Join a selected fit's
   local measurements to the same geometry in its finished track, then inspect
   exact contact frames, velocity, ownership, and the following interval.
   Diagnose measurement drift, insufficient delivery, and continuation
   separately. Match states by their actual prefix, never just gap number.
3. **Prove authority away from the search.** A physical proposal starts from
   recorded exact states, retains a solid control, and is replayed through
   current contact, following contact, and complete continuation. A learned
   proxy or local Pareto win alone is insufficient. Use a fixed basis that
   tests an equation or missing state variable, not successive dose walks.
4. **Price integration separately.** Measure both useful retained changes and
   charged frames. Where integration costs probes or displaces a candidate,
   include a matched control that pays that same cost without the physical
   change. A gain with zero retained intervention proves no physical benefit.
5. **Validate once before the governed experiment.** Freeze the mechanism,
   source defaults, evidence schedule, and expected causal signature before
   opening validation. Failed validation closes that version. A revision
   needs new independent causal evidence and a fresh declared cohort.

Use at most two independent mechanisms per tranche. A diagnostic has an explicit
question and a finite collection; do not continue collecting merely because a
result is disappointing. Keep a concise decision ledger here and summarize
milestones in `compiler-improvement-campaign.md`. Raw tracks, state banks, and
bulk per-contact reports belong under `generated/`, with checksums.
