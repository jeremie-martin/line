## Contract

The scorer, Golden evaluator, authored beat targets, case catalog, budget
identity, validity rule, qualification rule, and promotion inference are frozen.
No target forgiveness, case-specific behavior, benchmark-budget branching, or
general kinematic planner is in scope. A candidate may shape an already-authored
contact transition, but may not rewrite the meaning of that contact.

The initial acceleration-line deferral was a phase boundary, not a permanent
ban. It held while passive carrier, auxiliary-contact, response-admission,
repair, and search-allocation mechanisms still had untested causal leads. Those
families are now exhausted below 650. Under the owner's explicit instruction
to resolve blockers autonomously and continue until the headline target is met,
the campaign therefore opens one narrowly bounded active-material primitive:
forward tangential acceleration on a reserved fraction of ordinary normal
post-contact candidates, only for whole segments beginning inside the frozen
`IMPACT_WINDOW * contactSpeed` episode. Geometry, active collision normal,
entry, late ride-out, candidate count, exact evaluator, ranker, scorer, targets,
and solid controls remain unchanged. This is an explicit scope transition;
general trajectory synthesis and kinematic planning remain out of scope.

The superseded 596.9655 baseline's exact counterfactual replay is the economic prior, not promotion
evidence. Multiplying impact residuals by 0.75 replays to **651.51** (+54.54).
The 0.28–0.40 authored-target band carries about +33 points; perfect feasible
delivery carries about +42. Both ignore cross-axis prices and therefore cap
expectations rather than certify a mechanism.

The frozen pre-promotion aggregate atlas is `impact-delivery-650-baseline-atlas.md`; regenerate
it with `npm run study:impact-atlas`. Its JSON companion binds the exact baseline,
archive commitments, all 194,688 retained observations, and the fixed follow-up
cohorts without duplicating the 178 MB row payload already committed by the
verified archive.

The first mechanism family is capture–redirect–release: a short passive contact
surface captures the incoming trajectory, a continuation redirects along the
authored contact normal/tangent, and the natural end releases the rider. It is
an additive ordinary-pool proposal behind a default-off experiment flag. The
existing planner, ranker, readiness model, redraw-dose law, and repair machinery
remain available as controls.

Closed search-side veins are not silently reopened: base forward-evaluation
depth, width/dose ladders, pace terms, continuation filters, aim-center reuse,
and tail-lane slack. Earlier broad acceleration arms also remain closed. The
new contacted-window material arm is licensed by the accumulated-contact ruler
and the measured passive ceiling; it is not a retune of those old arms.
