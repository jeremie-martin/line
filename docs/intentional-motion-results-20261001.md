# Intentional motion: production delivery and measured results

Delivered on 2026-10-01. The numerical ambition remains open; the implementation,
complete evaluation and integrated review collection are delivered.

The shared compiler now produces context-sensitive arrangements, deliberate
support-to-receiver transfers and substantially reduced unwanted speed bursts.
It retains functional folds, S sweeps, ripples, terraces, scatter and open arcs
with normal lines and unchanged native physics.

**Frozen V6 improves from 536.3321 to 835.6161 (+299.2839), with all 460 canonical
rides valid and fully fulfilled. The 850 numerical ambition remains unfinished.**
The twelve-track production collection passes every frozen motion condition.
The remaining accuracy and motion limitations are recorded below.

## Work and intentional variety

All proposals, unsuccessful branches, recovery and compiler verification share
one **3,000,000-frame allowance**. Canonical median work is **2,198,910 frames**,
mean **2,075,492**, 95th percentile **2,561,115**, and maximum **2,999,997**.
The measured median compiler-call time is **84.3 seconds**, with a 95th percentile
of **130.9 seconds**, on a shared concurrently loaded host. Independent judging
has a separate median of about **0.19 seconds**. Imports, rendering and audio
processing are not included in those compiler-call times; these are observations,
not isolated latency guarantees. Confirmation uses a median 2,314,650.5 frames
and 91.4 seconds per compiler call. The complete 20-worker confirmation run took
23m 33.6s wall time. GNU time reports 2.45 GiB maximum RSS for the run; this is
not summed concurrent pool memory or an isolated compiler-only measurement.

Across the frozen canonical automatic plans, requested support shares are
**26.7% open arcs, 55.3% guided connected shapes, 16.9% scatter and 1.1% ordinary
startup**. Guided requests include **891 later-receiver transfers** alongside
3,022 paired requests. Duration shares and all construction/context groups are
retained in the detailed evidence. Every one of these requests is realized;
fulfillment does not certify continuous sliding, every bend's contact or beauty.

The authored calm subset contains 132 support requests across four musical
parents: 94 open, 29 guided and nine scattered. Calmness changes preferences
without banning functional guidance. This limited subset and the known-song
opening panel do not establish broad generalization to every kind of quiet music.
The same seeded choice stream is shared across related plans, so support counts
are not thousands of independent artistic trials.

## What changed in the compiler

The public compiler now combines a context-sensitive V2 arrangement policy with
shared physical search. Authored impact and speed inform quiet versus energetic
choices; repeated phrases, broad preferences and an independent seeded choice
stream preserve variety. Plans are fixed before search, and increasing budget
cannot reroll a difficult request. Existing V1 and ordinary profiles remain
reproducible for V5 and V4.

Connected constructions now support a deliberate lower support → flight → later
upper receiver layout, alongside the familiar paired form. The receiver proposal
uses the rider's observed free-flight body pose. Functional checks include all
body contacts and the rider's actual position beyond the lower support. Folds and
terraces retain contacted corners, and smooth shapes retain meaningful reversals.
Scattered fragments retain their distinct physical representation. Every physical
line is normal type 0, with unchanged native physics.

Search evaluates gravity-separated speed corrections over several timescales,
including windows crossing a support boundary. It also considers motion after a
quiet landing. Contact-aware proposals, guide orientation, compact fold geometry,
construction-specific response memory and bounded previous-transition revision
make better candidates available to that objective. A small ending pass protects
the completed incumbent and spends only the shared remaining allowance.

The separation is deliberate: the policy chooses the requested construction,
search realizes it, and independent checks measure what actually happened.
Music, motion, fulfillment and work remain separately inspectable. There is no
song-name exception, timestamp fix, hidden acceleration segment or easier-plan
fallback. See the [architecture guide](production-repertoire-architecture.md).

## Motion qualification and its limits

All twelve predeclared production tracks complete and fulfill their requests.
Independent cold native replays reproduce the saved trajectories exactly. Across
the complete collection, duration-normalized integrated excess above the frozen
25/100/250 ms speed-gain bands falls by **97.85%, 98.92% and 98.93%** relative to
the prior automatic collection. The [qualification archive](evidence/intentional-motion-production-20261001.json.gz)
retains the successful and unsuccessful collections and the failing baseline self-check.
This is a reduction in excess burst burden, not
98% less total acceleration or movement. Gravity-assisted speed and intentional
impacts remain part of the ride.

## What the research ruled in and left open

The [search archive](evidence/intentional-motion-search-20261001.json.gz) preserves
959 outcomes from 55 studies, including negative results and failed launches.
The early calibration and contract pilots are preserved separately. The deployed
[proposal-memory provenance](evidence/repertoire-search-memory-v6-provenance.json)
records 14,340 development demonstrations and their sources. Canonical performance
is an optimized development result; every learned proposal still receives native
physical validation. Several ideas
received revised implementations after their first limitation became clear:
receiver geometry, fold engagement, proposal coverage, response weighting,
continuation planning and completed-track repair.

More training demonstrations were not uniformly helpful. Broader native lookahead,
restricting the historical arrival-value model and repeatedly rebuilding long
suffixes also produced material mixed regressions. Whole-track re-fitting often
spent the remaining allowance without improving a complete track. The small final
support pass was more economical on its declared pilot and received a full trial.
Duplicate-offer suppression makes proposal slots available for fresh controls;
its counter alone does not establish net physics-frame savings.

The 750k/1.5m/3m/5m study keeps requests fixed and preserves all outcomes. More
budget is not monotonically better, because it changes search choices as well as
available refinement. No broad budget increase was used to obtain the headline.
Unadopted repair/value prototypes are preserved at `55f774fa` on
`research/intentional-motion-repair-20261001`. They are research evidence, not
extra default compiler paths.

The remaining numerical opportunity is primarily complete automatic musical
accuracy and preparation of useful arrival states. Fixed-window scores include
ordinary surroundings and cannot establish equally high local accuracy for every
construction. Context tables are descriptive associations across different targets
and entry states, not causal rankings of interchangeable shapes. Neither an initial
failure nor this campaign's result establishes a geometry or search ceiling.
