### What we are trying to make intentional

The owner welcomes lower strikes, separate upper receivers, scattered transfers,
strong expressive motion, and potentially inverted sled riding. Guidance can
substantially change the trajectory while preparing a later strike. The result
should make the intended musical interaction clear, without flattening the ride
or requiring an artist to specify each rail manually.

the questions "what happened?", "which musical request did it serve?", and

- Preserves the interpretation of known clean lower landings, including the

- Distinguishes the named lower-to-upper transfer without depending on whether

- Recognizes that upper contact can be useful steering or a musical strike;
  contact location alone does not choose its artistic role.

- Detects displaced problems in the following passage, and does not reward

### Outcome and working scope

Aim to deliver **a more general, physically meaningful definition of impact,
integrated into a high-performing automatic compiler that produces clearer
musical strikes and varied complete tracks**. Develop, compare and challenge the
definition before optimizing the compiler against it. Measurements, construction,
search and independent review must describe the same physical interactions.
Preserve the successful lower landings and expressive variety already achieved.
A successful isolated detector or hand-arranged clip does not finish the campaign.

The author supplies musical times, desired impact strengths, other motion targets
and broad creative preferences. Search and automatic arrangement choose how to

profiles are observed outcomes and diagnostic examples; they are not new required
authoring concepts or a prescribed sequence of behaviors. Existing geometric
preferences still express visual variety. The compiler remains free to discover

impact definition. Its shared implementation should make later geometry easier
to introduce without adding a new impact rule for each construction.

### 2. Develop and challenge a general impact definition

The central research task is the meaning and measurement of impact itself:
identify a physically meaningful strike, its onset and its strength, regardless
of whether the contacted geometry lies above, below or beside the rider. Seek a
small, coherent definition that explains the evidence across constructions and
can guide optimization. Do not encode the panel's descriptive labels as separate
event types or patch each failure with its own formula. Ordinary contact and
continuous steering should not become repeated impacts simply because the engine
reports collisions, while a clear upper strike must not be invisible because it
fails a lower-landing convention.

Keep the actual contact history and time-resolved rider response together. Extend
the existing observation path rather than creating separate physics replays for
each diagnostic. Inputs can include contacting rider points, geometric contact
location/direction, free flight, whole-body and sled response, and duration.
Construction IDs and the words "main" or "guide" remain provenance, not definitions
of an impact. A meaningful response cannot be determined solely by the first
contact frame or by a global peak floor.

Implement and compare a small number of mechanistically distinct candidate
definitions, including alternatives to the current fixed-window redirection
quantity. Investigate physically bounded response accumulation, meaningful changes
in motion around contact, and ways to distinguish an onset from ongoing support;
these are research directions, not preselected formulas. The existing calibrated
definition and its successful examples are a serious baseline. Keep the useful
physical reasoning and evidence even if the final formula or event recognition
changes substantially.

Each iteration must expose actual event sequences, timing and strength on complete
saved rides, not just produce a better aggregate number. Inspect disagreements
in native replay, vary conditions in bounded experiments, examine the physical
cause of failures and revise the candidate. Expand beyond the sixteen known
passages using declared development inputs, preserving reserved runs for later
validation. A definition's weak first implementation does not end the idea; nor
does a successful fit to those sixteen passages establish generality. Retain an
explicit uncertain result where the evidence does not distinguish alternatives.

Verify the known clean multi-peak landing, the two-surface Tiki interaction,
the tiny-touch/strong-response Amour example, quiet contacts, sustained steering,
simultaneous contacts and contact interruptions. Check boundary placement,
threshold sensitivity, observation-coordinate transformations and independence
from provenance labels. Segment identities alone must not produce impacts;
physical changes caused by retessellation must be measured rather than assumed
away. Preserve native frame conventions and gravity separation.

The current impact window is six native frame intervals, evaluated inclusively
at seven timestamps. A short response can already contribute strongly; merely
shortening that window is not the objective. Preserve the successful redirection
measurement where it describes one coherent strike, while preventing a later,
separate receiver from contributing to it. Conversely, test whether distributed
turning/body contact can accumulate a misleadingly strong value, as the owner
reports for passage 12. Contact posture and motion are explanatory observations,
not grounds for globally excluding inverted or upper strikes.
Passages 12 and 2 measure similarly (0.689 versus 0.674) despite very different
owner judgments of strike clarity; retain that direct comparison. Passage 13
also has an inverted configuration but is preferred to 12. Neither a larger
response peak nor an upright-pose rule is established as the solution.

Keep event identity, timing and strength separately inspectable while developing
one shared impact contract. Strength is part of this research, not an unchanged
scalar bolted onto a new detector. Test whether the response being accumulated
belongs to one strike and whether its magnitude corresponds to the intended
notion of impact. Preserve useful lower-landing calibration where supported, and
check its applicability to other contacts rather than applying it automatically.
Use the broader existing calibration evidence and new physical comparisons;
label any uncalibrated scale honestly. Merely matching all authored beats or
reducing the number of unmatched observations cannot select the definition.

Version and record a working measurement definition before using it to optimize
the compiler, then evaluate the existing compiler under that definition. A later
definition revision requires a new baseline and new comparisons under the same
revision. This prevents moving the ruler during search. It is a research contract
until the evidence and artistic review support production adoption.

### 3. Test the definition through physical construction experiments

Use saved incoming states and the existing connected/scattered builders to make
controlled variations in approach, contact and release. These experiments both
challenge the candidate definition and establish that useful physical alternatives
are realizable. They are not a commitment to implement a catalogue of named
motion types. Important comparisons include:

1. A coherent strike and a similar accumulated response spread across separate
   engagements: the definition must not credit a later interaction to the first.
2. A distinct strike and substantial smooth redirection: both can change the
   trajectory, but the resulting musical impact measurement must remain meaningful.
3. Different physical realizations of the same authored impact, including an
   upper contact: timing, strength and continuation must be checked under the same
   rule. An existing off-beat collision cannot become correct merely by naming it.

If these experiments expose a failure in the definition, return to stage 2 and
revise it before committing search to that objective. Understanding whether the
measurement or the available construction/search is inadequate is part of the
campaign, rather than a reason to push a failed formula into production.

For guidance, check cumulative speed gain over the entire engagement as well as
individual bursts. The rejected passage 10 gains about 27% body speed while
gravity slows it; guide friction is the positive term in a replay-verified
collision-update decomposition. A chain of small responses can therefore be
unacceptable even without one large jolt. Passage 15 adds a local speed-up that
would be hidden by the net correction over its longer passage. Preserve the
whole-interval and local observations together, without making every physical
correction or useful contact undesirable.

Test across different incoming speeds, approach angles, musical spacing and
construction contexts. Compare alternate contact preparations and receiver
placements from the same incoming state. Reuse causal deletion/perturbation
probes where they clarify attribution. Every locally promising result must be
replayed from the unchanged prefix and continued through subsequent contacts;
a local scalar gain can otherwise conceal a broken or musically worse suffix.

Use these experiments to test the current architectural assumption that an
interval's opening support contact owns the musical event. If necessary, allow
shared search over neighboring intervals to choose which physical engagement
fulfills the authored target. A constrained upper-contact assay may establish
capability, but its hand-imposed condition must not silently become a production
policy. Preserve the distinction between what was observed and what search can
reliably produce; one briefly inverted frame does not demonstrate sustained
upside-down riding. Additional capabilities remain optional extensions once the
general impact objective and its automatic realization work.

### 4. Integrate the definition and improve the automatic compiler

Integrate the promising interpretation and physical proposals through the common
repertoire/search path, using the existing control registry, contact observer,
receiver builder and bounded neighboring-interval refinement where appropriate.
Factor shared observations once and give the independent whole-track checker
the same definition, with tests against cold native replay. Avoid accumulating
separate song/geometry penalties and duplicate evaluators.

Connect the selected impact definition to event recognition, one-to-one musical
association, strength error and handling of extra impacts, local candidate ranking,
whole-track evaluation and the saved review. A newly recognized off-beat strike
must participate in the same musical accounting as an ordinary strike; it must
not remain an informational marker while search continues optimizing only old
landings. Conversely, contact without a meaningful strike should remain usable
for smooth guidance. Validate the complete path, including detection and matching,
rather than changing only the reported magnitude.

The current compiler rejects extra detected `landing` events
(`arc_motion.ts`, candidate `offbeat` check; `arc_refinement.ts`, whole-track loss),
and `buildDriftReport` reports only that event class as off-beat landings. That
supports the expected mechanism: making a previously invisible strike part of
the shared impact account can make its incorrect timing matter to optimization.
It does not by itself guarantee that current proposals can find a good replacement.
Measure that distinction and improve construction/search where needed.

Allow automatic arrangement and search to discover how to satisfy the specification.
An upper strike may result from the available geometry, the incoming state or an
automatic creative choice; it does not require a new compulsory upper-hit request
or sampling policy. Retain existing broad geometry preferences and seeded variety,
without preassigning a diagnostic behavior to every beat. Artists should not have
to assign each rail or author extra beats. Musical requests and explicit creative
requirements remain fixed during matched comparisons: budget growth or search
failure cannot reroll a difficult plan into an easier one. Distinguish any
justified arrangement-policy change from improvement to the realization of
unchanged requests.

Direct search toward the physical response associated with the intended beat,
while evaluating the incoming approach, guidance and following musical passage.
Do not obtain a better impact number by borrowing a later collision. Distinguish
off-beat contact from an off-beat impact under the common definition: useful
transfers and continuous steering remain possible, while a clear extra strike
must be accounted for. Unresolved perceptual cases are not automatic penalties.
Targeted improvements should come from better geometry and coordinated approach,
contact and release, as well as appropriate candidate selection.

Use small paired experiments for development, then expand the promising changes.
Reuse verified observations and immutable prefixes. Meter every proposal, failed
attempt, observer and reconstruction inside the shared compiler allowance;
report independent judgment separately. Profile overhead before adding expensive
work to every speculative branch. The previous impact campaign demonstrated
that promising local searches can exhaust the full-track budget when invoked
indiscriminately; carry that evidence forward.

Keep normal type 0 lines, native physics, meaningful shape engagement and visual
variety. Reject apparent progress obtained by turning every passage into open
flight, flattening the shapes, deleting welcome receivers or producing repetitive
easy tracks. Cleanup should accompany changed ownership and retired paths, not
expand into an unrelated repository rewrite. A simpler coherent design can
justify a small measured tradeoff; inspect it over enough paired runs rather
than rejecting it over a one-point fluctuation.

## The outcome

Given music, a seed and broad preferences, the production compiler should make
a varied, accurate ride whose motion fits the passage: smooth when the music
calls for calm, expressive when it calls for energy, with convincing transitions.
Preserve the strong folded shapes, scattered interruptions, S sweeps, ripples,
terraces and open arcs that made the latest collection successful. Add deliberate
transfers from a sliding support to a later control rail as another useful option.

The numerical ambition is **at least 850 on a frozen V6**, alongside measured
improvement in unwanted speed bursts and calm-passage motion. These are separate
requirements: a higher musical score cannot excuse worse motion, and a quiet but
inaccurate or visually impoverished ride is not success. This is a research target,
not a claim that its feasibility has already been demonstrated.

Keep normal type-0 lines and the native physics unchanged. Useful steering,
gravity-driven acceleration, purposeful flight and strong musical impacts remain
welcome. The objective is neither minimum movement nor minimum control-rail use.
Do not flatten distinctive shapes, turn functional geometry into decoration,
or silently substitute easier constructions to improve a score.

Approval should authorize the whole connected campaign. The stages below are
internal milestones, not repeated permission requests or stopping points after
one successful experiment. Further owner feedback remains welcome; the final
integrated collection is the planned artistic review point.

The motion evidence comes from four known songs, not an unseen music population.
Control-rail removal is a diagnostic, not a validated replacement compiler.
Guide-only contacts can also boost speed: separating the rails is an artistic
opportunity, not by itself a solution to the acceleration issue.

## 1. Define a small, useful motion contract

Turn the existing investigation into reusable compiler observations and independent
evaluation. Reuse the measured physics identities rather than starting another
open-ended telemetry project.

Measure speed gain separately from gravity along the actual trajectory, direction
changes, landing behavior and later contact activity. Keep contact role and musical
timing visible. Inspect short bursts and cumulative passage behavior so spreading
one impulse over more frames cannot hide the same problem. Report absolute and
relative speed changes; neither alone is reliable at every riding speed.

Use the cheap native trajectory information for routine candidate evaluation.
Check its frame alignment and results against the detailed reference-engine
observer on a small panel. Per-collision forensic replay remains a diagnostic;
it must not become a second expensive physics pass for every search candidate.
Body-speed residuals are descriptions of motion, not proof of mechanical energy
creation or artistic failure in every frame.

Build a compact calibration panel from:

- The three owner-reported bursts, with their full musical surroundings.
- The positively reviewed ordinary Luna and Tiki openings, alongside all three
  automatic seeds, including the relatively smooth Tiki 101 case.
- Gravity-driven acceleration, strong intended landings, substantial guided
  shapes, scattered transfers and unguided passages that must remain possible.
- Further calm, energetic and transition passages selected from specifications
  by their authored targets, before inspecting their compiler outcomes.

Use existing feedback as the artistic anchor. Other examples are controls or
provisional judgments, not invented owner approvals. Determine which observations
can distinguish the reported problems without rejecting useful motion. Start
with interpretable limits or tradeoff rules; do not invent one universal beauty
penalty or equate high requested impact with permission for arbitrary speed boosts.

**Deliverable:** a documented, tested motion summary; a saved calibration panel;
and explicit acceptance bands and comparison rules for bursts, calm passages and
energetic controls. Values are established during this stage and frozen before
canonical optimization. If a proposed measure fails the controls, revise or drop
that measure rather than claiming it certifies the desired behavior.

## 2. Make arrangement and rail layout intentional

Develop a small production-policy revision and bounded layout prototypes before
freezing their evaluation contract. Keep one shared construction plan and search.

### Musical context

Use existing impact and speed targets, nearby target changes, gap duration and
authored phrase boundaries to inform arrangement. Broad creative preferences and
a separate seeded random stream still create variety. No new per-beat authoring
requirement, song-name rules, hardcoded introductions or large emotion taxonomy.

Calm passages should favor choices that allow smooth traversal; this can include
open arcs and suitable guided shapes. Energetic passages retain a broad repertoire.
Transitions must consider the next musical demand, rather than sacrificing a quiet
passage to an abrupt last-moment correction. Repeated constructions over short
phrases remain useful, with deliberate contrast between phrases.

Keep the responsibilities clear: policy chooses the intended construction and
motion expectations; search chooses concrete geometry and entry/exit states.
Record the context, eligible choices, selected request and actual realization.
The planner must not secretly reroll its request when search finds it difficult.
Requests remain identical when only the compilation budget changes.

### A later control rail

Treat paired and separated rails as layout options within existing constructions,
not six new primitive families. Retain full paired shapes where they create the
strong visual form the owner likes. Develop a deliberate support → flight → later
control interaction, including a real interval with no lower support beneath it.

Begin with arc, S and ripple examples whose exploratory trimming retained the
existing shape contract. Then develop folded and terraced variants deliberately:
define where their meaningful corners or reversals remain and how the rider
engages with them. Removing a required lower corner and keeping the old label
is not sufficient. Test selected outgoing states through the following passage,
not only through the transfer itself.

Use the existing scattered construction as a comparison and keep its distinct
fragment-based representation. A scattered passage is not automatically equivalent
to a connected guided shape, and its contacts need the same motion scrutiny.

**Deliverable:** reproducible context-sensitive plans, explicit layout requests
and functional checks, with native examples across different musical demands.
Publish unsupported or unsuccessful cases. A first failure prompts diagnosis and
another meaningful approach; it does not establish that a layout is impossible.
Avoid shipping nominal new styles with no substantial physical or visual difference.

## 3. Freeze V6 before the main optimization campaign

**Recommend V6 for the changed arrangement and layout task, not to make the music
arbitrarily harder.** Preserve V5 and V4, including their checks and stored results.
Do not compare different benchmark numbers as if they measured the same task.

V6 should retain the existing musical judge, timing, impact definition and normal
physics. Keep the familiar musical-score × construction-fulfillment headline and
report both factors separately. Use separate motion qualification and diagnostics
alongside that headline; do not multiply it by an unvalidated aesthetic score.
Every scheduled musical/construction result remains in the headline even if its
motion fails qualification. Motion failures cannot disappear through filtering.

Keep two equally weighted panels:

1. Fixed construction and layout requests across musical contexts. These test
   whether the compiler can realize demanding requests, independently of what
   the automatic planner prefers to choose.
2. Complete automatic arrangements from the versioned context-sensitive policy.
   These test the actual product, including transitions and endings.

Reuse the current musical source distribution, adding only coverage needed for
calm-to-energetic transitions and the new layouts. Do not add exotic musical
demands or inflate easy cases. Balance by musical parent and construction family
so extra layout variants do not acquire disproportionate weight. Declare exact
cases, seeds, policy, plans, weights, motion checks and budget in the frozen
manifest. Author selection must be independent of candidate compiler scores.

The primary allowance remains **3 million actual compiler physics frames**,
including all search attempts and compiler verification. Freeze the benchmark
after the bounded contract pilot, before tuning against canonical V6 outcomes.
Measure an honest baseline: include the preserved compiler wherever the new
contract is representable, and a committed minimal integrated implementation for
the complete new task. Label unsupported requests and failures explicitly.

Reserve a new confirmation panel before optimization. Previously reported V5
confirmation is development evidence now. Additional seeds test variation, while
different musical inputs test broader transfer; report those distinctions and
duplicate trajectories. Reserve source selections where available and disclose
their exposure rather than calling familiar music unseen.

Plan for at least four arrangement seeds per canonical automatic case, subject
to the bounded pilot's resource measurement, and a disjoint confirmation seed
set. Prioritize musical/context coverage over multiplying identical runs. Freeze
the final sample sizes and any uncertainty-driven extension rule in the manifest;
do not stop or add seeds opportunistically when the headline crosses 850.

**Deliverable:** immutable V6 definition, baseline and new confirmation manifest.
The primary numerical target is **V6 ≥850**; it will not switch between V5 and V6
according to which result is higher. Report frozen V5 alongside it for direct
compiler comparison. The new motion bands are qualification conditions, not
claims that numeric checks replace the owner's visual judgment.

## 4. Improve contact realization, accuracy and useful work together

Run a sustained compiler campaign against the frozen task. Fix the geometry and
search mechanisms behind the motion, rather than relying on a penalty to find a
solution in the same limited candidate set.

Prioritize three connected experiment families:

| Work | Mechanism to investigate | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Contact geometry | Guide orientation, clearance, engagement depth, onset and extent; smooth entry/release; excessive repeated contacts. | Native trajectories with reduced unwanted bursts and retained steering, shape engagement and musical accuracy. Narrowing alone is not the assumed cause. |
| Entry and continuation | Prepare useful incoming states, search exits that serve the next beat, and allocate lookahead at difficult transitions. | Complete passages and tracks; an improvement must not merely move the burst or error to the adjacent support. |
| Search efficiency | Better proposals for each geometry, correctly scoped response reuse, measured allocation and incumbent-preserving repair. | Quality-versus-work curves, rejected-attempt accounting, completion rate and cost. Additional budget must buy useful work. |

Compare proposals and acceptance rules using paired fixed requests and seeds.
Keep musical loss, construction fulfillment and motion observations separately
visible while choosing a small production decision rule. Test contact-aware
proposals as well as motion-aware selection: a better objective cannot create
geometry the search never proposes. Preserve promising ideas for a second
informed implementation when the first exposes a correctable limitation.

Separate policy-only and realizer-only comparisons on compatible saved plans.
This distinguishes placing geometry in more suitable passages from genuinely
improving its physical realization. Report new-layout results separately where
the old compiler cannot represent the request. Calmness achieved by abandoning
expressive geometry everywhere must not masquerade as better contact control.

The ordinary arc and scattered constructors participate too; the reported bursts
are not exclusive to exotic connected shapes. Keep the named historical ordinary
profile reproducible for V4 comparisons. Share geometry and search infrastructure;
do not create a parallel motion-quality compiler or one optimizer per dashboard
example. Remove replaced or duplicated paths only after their role is understood.

Target complete automatic performance, difficult local errors and the three
current canonical completion failures, rather than polishing already strong fixed
rows. As scale context, an 850 V5 headline with its fixed panel unchanged would
require the automatic panel to rise from 606.8 to roughly 766.6. This illustrates
the size of the accuracy work; it is not a prediction of the V6 baseline.

Use targeted 750k / 1.5m / 3m / 5m curves on unchanged plans before increasing
compute broadly. Earlier higher-budget and repair studies were mixed; do not
repeat “more budget” as a mechanism by itself. Reuse immutable state and derived
observations, account for all attempts, and measure wall time and memory as well
as physics frames. Prefer strong first completions and productive improvements
over repeated whole-track work.

**Deliverable:** one improved production compiler, an experiment ledger with
positive and negative results, and matched evidence for motion, musical accuracy,
construction realization and cost. A small score tradeoff may be justified by a
substantial measured motion or design improvement; it must be disclosed and
investigated, not automatically vetoed or silently accepted.

## 5. Qualify the real product and deliver one coherent review

Freeze a committed candidate and run complete V6 canonical and newly reserved
confirmation panels. Run frozen V5 and the ordinary V4 companion, preserving
their definitions. If confirmation leads to another compiler revision, record it
as development feedback and reserve fresh confirmation rather than reusing the
same panel as untouched evidence.

Report headline and automatic/fixed components, raw musical scores, completion
and fulfillment failures, motion distributions and worst cases, individual
construction/context results, actual work and distinct tracks. Compare by matched
cases and seeds, and account for related musical parents rather than treating
every frame or repeated reference as an independent observation. Investigate
material regressions and execution errors; no failed runs may be omitted.

Update the existing production dashboard to use the actual public compiler and
new policy. Keep the same four songs × seeds 101/202/303 as a declared comparison
collection. Include the prior automatic and ordinary references, synchronized
native rider playback with music, shareable passage times and optional motion/
contact detail. Make the three reported bursts and Luna/Tiki openings easy to
compare, while showing the whole tracks and their energetic passages too.

Avoid turning the viewer into another research-only pipeline. Exercise seeking,
seed switching, playback, audio/track identity and mobile use. Preserve the
existing rendering command, but **new vertical videos are optional**; a complete
render batch is not a prerequisite for delivery or routine artistic review.

Delivery must show these outcomes together:

- The frozen numerical ambition, V6 ≥850, with the automatic panel and remaining
  failures visible. If unmet, state the actual result and the unfinished objective.
- Reduced unwanted burst magnitude and frequency under the predeclared motion
  rules, including individual follow-up of all three reported cases.
- Calmer passages improved against the appropriate references, without shifting
  their corrections to neighboring supports or weakening the musical targets.
- Retained energetic motion, distinctive geometry and substantial automatic
  variety; functional separated-rail layouts available through the real policy.
- Reproducible outputs and reasonable measured compilation cost within the shared
  allowance, with code, compact evidence and documentation committed and pushed.
  Large media and raw archives remain local.

Passing numerical checks supports a technical claim, not blanket artistic approval.
An early successful example is a milestone, not campaign completion; a high score
with unresolved motion failures is also unfinished. Deliver the integrated result
and its limits clearly so the owner can judge the next artistic questions from
real music, rather than isolated demonstrations.

## Scope and first action after approval

This campaign develops the current six constructions, their layouts, arrangement
and shared physical search. It does not add an unrelated primitive catalog, change
the engine, redesign music analysis, introduce mandatory detailed authoring, or
replace the dashboard wholesale. Extend the existing useful infrastructure.

First preserve the current compiler/library as the named baseline, assemble the
motion calibration panel, and begin paired contact-geometry and layout prototypes.
Those bounded experiments establish the contract for V6; then proceed through the
full compiler, qualification and product delivery without stopping for approval
at each internal milestone. Only a substantive conflict with the agreed product
direction should require reopening the roadmap.
