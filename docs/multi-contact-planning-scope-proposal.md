## Why this is the proposed next step

The September census establishes that impact underdelivery is already present
in the exact selected fit. Local response methods fail at useful gain.
Interrupting support creates useful impulse in two distinct physical states,
but immediate next-interval air-error cost is 3.95–73.44 times the saved
current-impact error. Reconstructing the return surface partly reduces that
cost and removes the useful impact gain. Those results support testing a
jointly realized transition over several authored intervals. They do not
prove that planning will succeed or that 650 is physically unreachable in the
current scope.

Prior work is not reset. The retired scalar speed-demand plan did not address
a binding resource; it will not be revived. Static two-capture menus,
capture-preserving release variants, and fixed local completion certificates
also remain closed. The new variable would be the coupled trajectory through
three contacts, optimized as one exact realized program with literal interval
ownership. Increasing the breadth of an existing candidate menu is not the
proposed mechanism.

## Bounded first study

- Use the promoted compiler snapshot and fresh discovery seed **260907002**
  on all 44 development sources. Capture one exact source run and select one
  interior three-contact window nearest one-third duration from baseline
  authored/physical facts. Keep unavailable cases explicit. Existing validation
  seeds 260907008–015 and all final confirmation seeds remain untouched.
- Implement one fixed three-contact solid-geometry solver. Its variables are
  contact position/phase, surface tangent, support extent, and release timing
  expressed in physical displacement, angle, and time units. Freeze its
  representation, bounds, solver, and stopping rule before reading outcomes.
  There is no source-specific policy or alternative-parameter menu.
- Every trial realizes all three transitions on one immutable-prefix engine.
  No independently invented boundary state is spliced into a track. Current
  event impact and outgoing interval air/speed/amplitude retain their literal
  authored ownership. Ordinary exact admission and the frozen full scorer
  remain authoritative.
- Compare with ordinary construction at the same measured physics-frame
  allowance. Limit the component work to **250,000 frames per source per arm**,
  at most **22 million component frames** total, plus the 44-source capture and
  its declared fidelity controls. This is an offline authority ceiling, not a
  proposed production allocation or a benchmark-budget identity branch.
- Count failed or incomplete programs explicitly. Report current and following
  interval errors, all contact and prefix checks, exact continuation, retained
  planned geometry, and complete scores against both the ordinary control and
  original incumbent. A local oracle or disappearance of the planned geometry
  cannot count as success.

One prototype and one frozen cohort are authorized by this proposal. A failed
authority or continuation gate closes it; there is no automatic seed, horizon,
solver, or compute expansion. A positive result must establish broad source
coverage and useful retained full-track gains before integration is considered.
Integration must then repay its charged work against a matched-cost control,
exercise its budget-dependent behavior at 150k and 1M–3M, and pass the existing
validation and governed N=8/16/32/48 workflow. No promotion is pre-authorized by
a component result.
