# Autonomous production repertoire: qualification and delivery

The compiler now makes complete varied musical tracks from a song, seed and broad
preferences. The production CLI, V5 and default dashboard use the same public
compiler. Guided folds, S sweeps, ripples, terraces, guided/open arcs and scattered
passages are requested explicitly, realized through shared search, and checked
against actual normal-line geometry and native trajectories. No showcase windows
or successful seeds were selected by hand.

**V5 improves from 449.0934 to 770.1170 (+321.0235). The ambition above 900 is not
achieved.** Complete automatic arrangements remain substantially less accurate
than the ordinary profile. This is a delivered production workflow with measured
improvement and disclosed failures, not a claim that the broader problem is solved.

## What improved, and what did not

Shared physical search replaces staged whole-source compilation and repeated
scattered suffix reconstruction. Construction checks participate in proposals
and continuations. A scattered interval is reconstructed locally and its actual
native exit state feeds the next interval. Reusable prefix observation avoids
duplicated work. Compatible geometry contexts get their own response memory.

The campaign also fixed a physical-outro normalization error, aligned search with
the authored construction windows used by the independent judge, and removed a
provably inactive smooth-bend coordinate for full folds. It hardened authored
input, audio-analysis and video identities, engine ownership and reference imports.
All physical lines remain normal type 0.

The final viewer verifies the saved audio checksum and plays an immutable blob of
those bytes. A historical ride cannot silently acquire a changed recording from
the same file path. A mismatch disables synchronized playback while preserving
native scrubbing; rapid selection also cancels superseded verification and media.

Evidence includes matched search/proposal studies, transfer across songs,
750k/1.5m/3m/5m budget curves, incumbent-preserving local repair, two unsuccessful
fold-coordinate implementations, and guide-removal counterfactuals. Larger budgets
and deeper/broader search were not reliably better. Local repair had modest gains
and substantial repeated work; it remains an explicit study rather than a default.
Unsuccessful experiments and their patch are retained in the campaign evidence.
Guide removal changed the ride in each tested construction, but that does not
prove that no alternative unguided geometry could work.

The remaining error is concentrated unevenly. For example, canonical **valid
automatic rides** have these descriptive per-construction errors:

| Requested construction | Speed RMS | Impact RMS |
|---|---:|---:|
| Open ordinary arc | 0.225 | 0.174 |
| Guided fold | 0.145 | 0.157 |
| Guided ordinary arc | 0.087 | 0.061 |
| Guided S sweep | 0.045 | 0.059 |
| Scattered | 0.047 | 0.071 |

These are associations across different incoming states and targets, **not causal
comparisons of interchangeable shapes**. Failed rides are excluded from this local
diagnostic, while remaining zero outcomes in the headline. Detailed evidence keeps
all axes, transitions, worst passages and request failures. Impact at the end of
a gap is attributed to the arriving support, not the preceding one.

This supports testing better arrival-state planning for open arcs and folds,
including how the preceding phrase prepares them. It does not justify silently
adding guides to open requests, flattening folds, or rerolling difficult plans.
The present experiments do not establish a geometry ceiling.

## Cost and intentional variety

Every compiler proposal, failed attempt, contact observation, reconstruction and
compiler replay fits inside one 3m physics allowance. Canonical fixed cases use a
median 2,136,954 frames; automatic cases use a median 2,693,010.5, with a maximum
2,999,978. The final automatic panel's compiler-call median is 92.0 seconds and
maximum 154.8 seconds under concurrent load. Imports before the call, independent
judgment and rendering are separate; these are observations, not wall-time guarantees.

The comparison holds the allowance fixed, not the work actually consumed.
Total canonical compiler work rises from 308.64m to 324.34m physics frames while
valid rides rise from 117 to 147. Eliminating repeated reconstruction does not
mean the whole improved compiler uses less work on every case; it can spend more
of the allowance successfully completing difficult arrangements.

Requested support shares in canonical automatic plans are 22.67% open arcs,
12.78% guided arcs, 15.78% folds, 11.03% S sweeps, 15.78% ripples, 6.86% terraces
and 15.09% scattered. Duration shares are also recorded. The nominal 30/55/15
phrase weights are not quotas: repetition suppression and finite seeded plans
change realized frequencies. The same seed shares its choice stream across songs,
with grouping adapted to the timeline and authored boundaries. These counts are
therefore correlated; they are not thousands of independent random trials.

The policy is deliberately a small first arrangement policy, not a learned model
of musical expression. Its requests stay fixed across budgets. V5 also freezes
those plans, so improvements cannot come solely from choosing easier shapes.
Future policy or realization semantics need explicit versioning rather than quiet
changes to this evaluation.

phrase boundaries are used; the V5 snapshots omit that metadata, so production

All 12 compile and fulfill their physical requests. Automatic compiler calls take
49–101 seconds; complete comparison processes take 56–135 seconds, including
reference handling and independent validation. Work and exact reference-cache
reuse are recorded separately. The largest music-library errors are in L’amour
de ma vie's speed and impact targets, despite fulfilled construction.

All twelve automatic rides have full finished vertical videos and excerpts.
Their ordinary comparisons reuse identical results where appropriate: there are
six distinct ordinary films, for **18 unique full films and 24 checked media
records**. Each full film and excerpt is 1080×1920 at 60 fps, includes actual music,
and passes full decoding. Rendering preserves the validated type-0 track geometry.
The [music inventory](evidence/production-repertoire-music-library-20261001.json)
records every file hash, duration, input identity, result and cache reference.

An eight-worker render trial exposed a separate resource defect: each Remotion
process could grow a decoded-frame cache based on a large share of machine RAM.
The batch was stopped when it caused heavy swapping. Rendering now bounds each
decoded-frame cache to 512 MiB and defaults to four compositor workers, with
explicit overrides. This is not a total memory cap for browsers and encoders.
The final collection is regenerated under the new pipeline identity; earlier
media remains under `generated/production-repertoire/library-pre-render-cache-bound/`.

## Reproduction and checks

Run the public workflow from [the production guide](automatic-production.md).
Canonical raw runs are `generated/production-repertoire/v5-qualified/`, reserved
confirmation is `v5-confirmation-qualified/`, ordinary qualification is
`v4-qualified/`, and the collection is `library-qualified/`. Each run's plan saves
the exact compiler checkout, frozen contract, seeds, allowance and runner identity.

`python3 scripts/benchmark/production_repertoire_evidence.py` verifies raw checksums
and emits compact qualification and media evidence. `--benchmarks-only` reports
qualification independently of rendering. It does not redefine or recompute the
benchmark's aggregation. `node scripts/gallery/check_production_ui.mjs` exercises
the completed library against the local dashboard server.

The final compiler's focused suite passes **30 tests in 13 files**, covering
physical construction, authored-window replay, determinism, endings, budgets,
engine ownership, reference imports, requests, cache identity and spectrum caching.
Three additional render-resource tests pass, and the spectrum-cache regression
passes again alongside them.
The repository-wide TypeScript check still has pre-existing errors; none were
reported in the changed files. This is not a claim that the entire repository
type-checks: the same 251 diagnostics remain, byte-for-byte unchanged.

The [final validation record](evidence/production-repertoire-validation-20261001.json)
includes all twelve native/audio/video comparisons, rapid switching, mobile layout,
phrase seeking, mutually exclusive players, changed-audio rejection and recovery,
and submitted creative settings. The browser range no longer rounds the default
guided balance; untouched preferences retain the exact policy value. Previous
exact job reuse, cancellation and low-budget incomplete-result checks are retained.
A cached render resume verifies all 24 media records in 3.08 seconds without
rewriting any of the 60 media/record files; its owned mirror starts and exits
correctly. Current compiler/engine identity matches the qualified runs.

Code, documentation and compact evidence are versioned. Raw archives, recordings,
intermediate captures and finished media stay local. Visual approval remains open
for one review of the integrated collection, especially repeated motifs, open/guided
balance, shape traversal, transitions and musical accuracy together.
