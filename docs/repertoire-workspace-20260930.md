# From isolated examples to a usable musical repertoire

The owner asked for sustained, ambitious execution across the agreed roadmap,
without stopping after each construction or treating every small improvement as
an independent milestone. This campaign follows all five strands of
[the roadmap](creative-repertoire-roadmap.md). Intermediate commits and experiments
are checkpoints, not automatic stopping points.

The intended result is a usable first creative workflow: several visibly useful
constructions, deliberate complete musical arrangements, understandable controls,
faithful inspection and finished videos, supported by shared efficient search.
Keep the positively reviewed scattered and folded character, the earlier pronounced
ripple and the subtler ripple. Accurate ordinary arcs remain the foundation.

## Connected work packages

1. **Shared search across constructions.** Diagnose and test initialization
   recovery at difficult shaped entries; avoid probing controls that provably
   cannot change the selected geometry; test a cheap physically evaluated source
   continuation before rebuilding an already-good ride. Account for all attempts,
   preserve eligible complete incumbents and compare validity, musical loss and
   work. Keep the production compiler and frozen benchmark contract unchanged
   unless an independently justified default change is explicitly made.
2. **Reusable composition.** Replace study-specific two-phrase wiring with a small
   separate composition plan. Support ordinary, shaped, guided/unguided and
   scattered passages in multiple orders, with repeated scattered passages and
   independently searched returns. Preserve the actual earlier state, all declared
   geometry choices and total work. Share the existing compiler and renderer;
   do not introduce a new music-specification language or an abstract motif system.
3. **A broader useful repertoire.** Develop complementary variations of the
   available physical constructions, including stronger ripples and candidates
   whose distinctive region the rider previously bypassed. Introduce further
   geometry when it offers a functional visual difference. Compare controls and
   actual motion; retain failures and meaningful retries. No universal contact
   quota or first-failure verdict defines a geometry's usefulness.
4. **Whole-track direction.** Build multiple contrasting arrangements using the
   same authored music, with different phrase lengths, order, intensity and return.
   Include guidance choices deliberately. Freeze useful recipes before a further
   reserved intervention. Technical success does not establish owner preference.
5. **A creative workspace.** Provide one clear place to browse the repertoire,
   inspect controls and existing examples, construct/save a composition, compile
   it with bounded work and review the exact resulting ride. Retain faithful
   native rider playback, comparison and finished production movies. Preserve
   provenance and previous results; do not present unsupported choices as success.

## Evidence and delivery

Use Luna and Amor for development and existing difficult ripple/fold entries for
search diagnosis. Reserve Tiki support starts in **24.0–26.5 s and 44.0–46.0 s**
before new recipe development. Tiki is a known song: this is another intervention,
not unseen-song evidence. Broader full-song arrangements are development examples.

Report a matrix of construction, passage, actual behavior, validity, local target
losses and physical work. Seeds with zero authored jitter are not independent
trials. Shared changes receive focused tests and default-behavior checks; gallery
and workspace operations receive real browser checks, including failure paths.

Deliver the integrated workflow, multiple complete musical examples and a candid
record of what remains exploratory. Keep normal lines only; freeze authored music,
benchmark, scorer, detector and physics. Commit/push code, plans and compact evidence.
Keep raw experiments, movies and the private transcript local. Owner feedback may
steer ongoing work, but new visual approval is not required for technical progress.

## Search decision after the controlled experiment

Twenty-four matched-ceiling calls compared inherited search, 80 generic recovery
proposals after failed initialization, removal of inactive fold coordinates, and
cheap replay of source controls. Recovery completes all three failed strong-ripple
or fold entries in this panel. Removing inactive coordinates changes search
allocation and materially worsens the Luna fold; every source-control preview
fails the requested quality/validity limit. Neither mechanism demonstrated a work
saving here.

Keep recovery in the composition workflow. The other two experimental options
are archived in commit `c2e05858`, with their full measurements retained; they are
removed from the delivered compiler surface. This is a result about these
implementations and passages, not a claim that the ideas cannot work. The normal
production configuration remains unchanged.

## Integrated delivery

All five work packages now have a connected implementation and empirical result.
The [workspace and results](repertoire-workspace-results-20260930.md) describe the
editable composition workflow, five finished musical arrangements, 16 construction
alternatives, 24 search calls, preserved failures, native/production checks and
unchanged default benchmark. The artist can now modify and compare complete
arrangements without another custom two-phrase study script.

Further creative development remains open. This checkpoint is a substantial body
of work for review, not a claim that every geometry, artistic choice or search
mechanism is solved. Keep working at this scope rather than returning to a sequence
of tiny isolated deliverables.
