# The ride camera lurches in spins (2026-10-31)

Jérémie saw the world lurch on the review page whenever the rider spins: an
unnatural forward acceleration, then a pull back. Examples: Trouble · dense ·
seed 101 at about 12.5 s and Trouble · runup · seed 202 at about 47.5 s
(`generated/trouble-variants-v2/dense-101`, `runup-202`).

This is a review-page change only. No compiler, measure, video or study change.

## Cause (confirmed)

- **Review page.** `production.js` centred the camera on the first traced point,
  `PEG`, a point on the sled. Read headless from the page's own line-renderer
  transform, the camera equals `PEG` to 1e-12 on both rides.
- **The spin.** PEG circles the body centre, the mean of the ten traced points,
  so the camera speeds up and slows down by 12–18% against the centre over each
  turn. The centre's speed stays smooth.

## Videos and study clips: affected, about a third as much

Finished videos and study clips (`scripts/export.ts`, `tools/measure/clip_render.ts`)
use the mirror's video-exporter camera. It was read headless from the exporter's
own `cameraFollower` on the same rides, without rendering. The mirror's physics
equals the saved trace exactly (maximum error 0).

- **What it follows.** `track.getRider(f).position`, the mean of six body points:
  BUTT, SHOULDER, RHAND, LHAND, LFOOT and RFOOT. The sled points (PEG, TAIL, NOSE,
  STRING) are left out, so this point sits off the body centre and circles it in
  a spin.
- **How it smooths.** It uses the follower step in mirror module 560 with its
  defaults: pull 0.01, push 0.8, roundness 0.5, and width and height scale 0.4.
  This is an elliptical soft dead zone around the camera, spanning about 40% of
  the frame (a little less at high zoom).
  - Inside the zone, the camera moves 1% of the gap per frame.
  - Near the edge, it is pushed along with the rider.
  - There is no time window. The camera at each frame comes from the previous
    camera and the current point. Videos are 60 fps, interpolated linearly
    between the 40 fps frames.
- **Zoom.** The spec lane through `createZoomer`, times 3.5 for clips
  (1280×720) and times 1.8 for vertical videos (1080×1920).
- **Measured without beat punch.** Finished vertical videos also add a
  per-beat zoom punch, which was not measured.

At speed the rider rides near the edge of the zone, so most of the six-point
mean's circling reaches the camera. The table isolates the spin-induced part: the
video camera against the same follower fed the ten-point centre.

| Window | Clip camera: speed vs the same follower on the centre | Vertical video camera |
|---|---|---|
| dense-101 spin, 12.40–14.00 s (1.72 turns) | +3.4% / −5.3%, extra acceleration up to 577 u/s² (RMS 176) | +2.8% / −4.5%, up to 420 u/s² (RMS 120) |
| runup-202 spin, 47.30–48.30 s (0.94 turns) | +2.3% / −4.6%, up to 839 u/s² (RMS 243) | +1.8% / −4.2%, up to 474 u/s² (RMS 159) |
| dense-101 ordinary, 31.00–32.60 s | +2.1% / −1.8% | +1.2% / −1.2% |
| runup-202 ordinary, 36.75–38.75 s | +2.2% / −1.0% | +1.7% / −0.9% |

Without the dead zone, the six-point mean itself swings +3.6% / −5.7% (dense) and
+3.0% / −4.8% (runup) against the centre in these spins. Clips are cut from full
renders, so their camera has the same history.

**Simplest video fix (not made).** Make the exporter's follower track the
ten-point centre. In the page, before rendering, replace
`cameraFollower._getRiderPosition(track, f)` with the mean of the ten named body
points of `track.getFrame(f).snapshot.entities[0].entities[0]`. This is one
function in `mirror/helper.js` `exportVideo`, after `waitForVideoExporterReady`.
The video path is hashed into study and render identities, so this changes every
render identity. The published pair studies keep their clips.

## Review-page fix

`scripts/gallery/native/camera.js` gives the review page the videos' camera rule,
with that one defect removed. It runs the mirror's own follower step (module 560,
used verbatim through the gallery bundle, default settings) on the ten-point body
centre. It uses the canvas size in CSS pixels and the page's zoom slider.
- **Seeking.** The path is computed from frame 0 for the current view, so a seek
  shows what playback from the start would show.
- **Between frames.** The camera is interpolated linearly between frames, as in
  the mirror.
- **No new settings.**

`view.js` now places the camera itself, and `production.js` passes only the view
size, zoom and time. `production.html` is the only page that draws rides:
- pairs and night show pre-rendered videos (unchanged);
- the spec dashboard has no ride view;
- contact-impact-review and interaction-review are panels of `production.html`.

## Before and after

Measurement setup:
- Headless Chromium at 1440×1000. The canvas is 1323×418 CSS px, at the default
  zoom 1.4.
- The camera is read from the transform the page draws with, at every frame
  from 0, reached by seeking.
- The body centre is the mean of the ten traced points. Speeds are central
  differences and accelerations second differences, at 40 fps.
- The after camera equals the offline follower on the centre to 1e-12.

Camera speed against body-centre speed, largest excess and deficit:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| dense-101 spin | +17.0% / −12.3% | +4.6% / −2.3% | +4.4% / −4.5% | +6.9% / −8.7% |
| runup-202 spin | +17.7% / −13.8% | +3.3% / −1.9% | +3.7% / −4.1% | +6.1% / −2.9% |
| dense-101 ordinary | +5.7% / −5.2% | +7.5% / −2.8% | +5.0% / −2.1% | +10.1% / −3.7% |

less than the videos do. It does not fall further behind, and the rider stays
well inside the frame. Landing jolts reach the world about half as hard as
before, as in the videos.

## Not verified

- **Whether he sees it.** No one has checked whether Jérémie still sees a lurch
  after the change, or whether the videos' 3–5% residual is visible to him.
- **Live playback.** Only seeking was measured headless. Live playback uses the
  same function per animation frame, but it was not watched in a browser.
- **Beat punch.** The vertical videos' beat punch was not measured. It changes
  the zoom, and with it the dead zone, at each beat.
- **Other rides and settings.** Only two rides were measured, at the page's
  default zoom and one canvas size. A different zoom or window size changes the
  dead zone, as it does in the mirror's own editor.

Measurement scripts are kept out of the repository (one-off). They read the
mirror's exporter camera in-page, capture the page's line-renderer transform,
and compute the metrics above from the saved traces.
