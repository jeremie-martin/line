# Toward 6,500 ns per charged frame: no promotion this round

The accepted production source remains `0b5402df`, with its qualified default
confirmation median of **6,979.3 ns per charged physics frame**. The new target
is below 6,500 ns. It is not reached in this round. No experimental production
code, candidate-only tests, runtime options or packaged binaries remain.

Three separate frozen candidates passed their initial correctness checks but
failed their declared fixed twelve-pair speed screens. None proceeded to fresh
confirmation. These intervals request 95% confidence, with exact coverage 96.14%.

| Candidate | Median paired change | Interval | Declared speed margin |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| [Reuse exactly equal invalidated observations](engine-observation-equality-20261106.md) | −0.370% | [−1.031%, +0.567%] | 0% |
| [Schedule independent scalar constraint factors](engine-scalar-pairs-20261106.md) | +0.093% | [−0.471%, +1.003%] | 0.75% |
| [Use the norm's exact unit term](engine-unit-norm-20261106.md) | +0.517% | [−0.107%, +0.780%] | 0% |

All three verdicts are INCONCLUSIVE. Correctness and simpler arithmetic alone
are insufficient evidence of a speed improvement. Rejected implementations and
their validations remain in the linked research records; production is restored.
The full structural gate was not rerun for rejected candidates after restoration
because every executable and packaged artifact is byte-identical to the already
qualified source. Candidate validation scope is stated separately in each record.

The [new scoped CPU/allocation profiles](engine-post-prefix-profile-20261106.md)
show raw-observation allocation declining after the accepted prefix change.
Ordinary native stepping remains about 29% of sampled compiler time. Within its
optimized function, [instruction samples](engine-instruction-profile-20261106.md)
attribute about 53% to constraints, 28% to collision visits and 15% to cell lookup
and filtering. These are diagnostic samples, not independent timing experiments.

The [collision work audit](engine-collision-work-20261106.md) rejects three more
mechanisms before implementation: exact repeated miss positions are too rare,
duplicate failed-line reuse covers only 14–15% of visits, and testing segment
position first increases estimated multiplication work by 28–29%.

Two boundary checks also rule out apparent opportunities. The frame-index getter
accounts for only 0.267 ms of 3,330.722 ms of scoped CPU samples (about 0.008%);
this does not justify a new metering ABI. The generated ordinary solver branches
to the next line at offsets 0x16d2 and 0x16ec before loading or calculating the
along-segment coordinate at 0x16f2–0x1714. LLVM/V8 already avoids that arithmetic
on perpendicular misses despite its earlier placement in Rust source. No source
rewrite or measurement claim follows from either observation.

The next substantial gain needs a new mechanism for avoiding meaningful solver
or compiler work with an exact equivalence argument. The profiles and closed
trial records provide that starting point; the acceptance policy and work
accounting are unchanged.
