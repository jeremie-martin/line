# General impact campaign — delivery

Selected compiler: **`7cbf50c329b041e1133090cd05af07d5c5734593`**.
Working measurement: **`line.contact-impact.v1`**.
The [six-stage roadmap](intentional-motion-roadmap.md#active-campaign-clear-musical-interactions-in-automatic-tracks)
connects physical investigation, a shared impact account, automatic construction,
paired evaluation and native musical review. This delivery is experimental;
the qualified production default remains available and unchanged.

## What changed

Actual contact and the rider's physical response now supply one shared account
for construction, local search, neighboring-interval refinement, complete-track
selection, independent native replay and the review. Contact can involve any
rider point. An upper receiver can therefore fulfill a musical request, and an
extra upper strike participates in the same timing and strength objective as a
lower strike. Segment names and posture do not prescribe separate event types.

The compiler combines one-frame contact preparation, opposing receiving-surface
proposals and coordinated interval search with a preference against sustained
non-gravitational speed gain. Musical inputs and seeded geometric requests stay
fixed. Every proposal and reconstruction uses the shared three-million-frame
allowance. Normal type-0 lines and native physics are unchanged.

The selected gain preference deliberately gives up about 15.4 experimental
quality points against a weaker preference to reduce measured excess gain by
about 92% on the development panel. This is a measured tradeoff addressing the
owner's rejected acceleration, not an improvement obtained by changing the ruler.

## Physical checks and limitations

An additional independent native replay covers all twelve candidate development
songs, their twelve automatic baselines and twelve ordinary references. All
36 saved rider traces reproduce exactly. Against the already improved automatic
baseline, one-frame excess burst burden falls **63.43%**; 100 ms and 250 ms burst
excess both reach zero. All three originally reported windows fall within the
frozen 100 ms band. Both quiet-opening motion comparisons satisfy their existing
ordinary-reference limits. These limits are numeric checks, not artistic approval.

The unchanged historical qualification as a whole **does not pass**: it also
requires the old landing validity and an additional reduction in old-definition
opening-impact error. Candidate tracks optimize the new interaction task, and
historical opening-impact RMS actually rises from 0.01544 to 0.03259. The
[full result and physical subchecks](evidence/general-impact-motion-qualification-20261002.json)
are preserved separately rather than relabeling that failure.

Remaining limitations matter:

- **Strength is not yet a fully calibrated perceptual measure.** Historical felt
  ranking is slightly worse than the established lower-landing definition. The
  rejected head-first Amor 202 contact still measures similarly to the approved
  clean lower landing. A tested whole-body coherence modifier does not repair
  that contrast and was not adopted.
- **Some quiet and strong contacts remain inaccurate.** Six of twenty songs have
  worse quiet-strength error than their paired baseline; several Tiki 303 strong
  requests are still weak. Aggregate timing gains do not erase those examples.
- **Continuous-contact resolution remains bounded.** A contact-free frame
  separates events, but two responses in ongoing contact inside the preceding
  six-step accumulation can remain one measured event. That is an explicit
  limitation, not a claim that they always feel like one strike.
- **Upper contact is demonstrated, not universally solved.** Opposing-surface
  proposals currently extend paired layouts. A useful body-side upper receiver
  is not evidence of sustained upside-down skiing.

These are reasons to retain an experimental contract and review the complete
rides. Any revised measurement needs a new identity, a remeasured baseline and
new paired evaluation before compiler tuning.

## Native review and reproduction

[Open the twenty-song native review](http://localhost:8767/motion-gallery/production.html?collection=/motion-gallery/impact-review.json&compare=previous).
Both sides are automatic arrangements evaluated under the same experimental
account. Playback uses the real rider, actual lines and verified music. The
measurement panel seeks directly to requested or observed interactions. Focused
passages include a beat-aligned upper receiver, a preserved clean lower landing,
quiet motion, the reduced acceleration and the unresolved head-first example.
Full songs remain available around every passage. No vertical videos were needed.

Generate an additional experimental song through the public compiler:

```sh
npm run produce:automatic -- --song=luna_bala_44s --seed=101 \
  --impact-contract=line.contact-impact.v1 --out=generated/my-impact-review
```

Omitting the impact-contract flag uses the validated compiler. Legacy report and
render/effect fields retain their historical landing meaning; the experimental
`contact-impacts.json`, saved account and native review use the new definition.
The generation form in the dashboard explicitly retains the validated compiler.
