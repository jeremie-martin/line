## Feedback to investigate before the next roadmap

- Sudden speed gains can look artificial even with normal type-0 lines. Distinguish
  gravity-driven acceleration and intentional expressive motion from abrupt gains
  associated with opposing rails. Do not equate normal-line validation with natural
  motion, or infer a cause solely from a rail's appearance.
- Explicit examples in automatic arrangements: L’amour de ma vie seed 303 around
  9.25–9.40 s; Tiki seed 101 around 15.32 s; L’amour de ma vie seed 101 around 5.95 s.
  Compare the ordinary references too: the concern predates the new repertoire.
- Calm, low-impact passages should retain the smoothness the owner values. The
  ordinary openings of Tiki and Luna look better in that respect than the automatic
  openings. Landing impact alone may not describe motion inside a support.
- Preserve variety and dramatic motion where appropriate. Do not minimize every
  motion diagnostic globally, remove all control rails, or flatten interesting
  constructions as a shortcut.
- Scattered passages sometimes transfer from sliding support to a later opposing
  rail, with no lower rail beneath that later interaction. The owner particularly
  likes this visual separation. Investigate whether connected constructions can
  offer a similar option; do not yet decide that this requires a new geometry
  family, a universal rule, or another specification layer.
- Full vertical rendering need not be required for future dashboard review.
  Faithful native playback with music can be the normal iteration surface; retain
  finished rendering when useful or requested.

First characterize the actual events and broader prevalence, check what existing
measurements capture or miss, and run bounded diagnostic experiments. Preserve the
current compiler, V5 and physics while doing this work. A future target around 850
or higher is an aspiration to consider in the next roadmap, not an already opened
score goal or authorization to change benchmark semantics silently. V6 is an option
to evaluate after measurement; it is not yet a decision.

The [completed investigation](motion-quality-investigation-20261001.md) records the
reported accelerations, their native collision mechanism, opening comparisons and
bounded separated-rail experiments. It supplies evidence for the next discussion;
it does not replace that discussion with an already approved roadmap.
