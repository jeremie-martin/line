# V7: the benchmark (design, 2026-11-01)

Agreed with the owner on 2026-11-01. The tooling is `npm run compare`
(ARCHITECTURE.md, Evaluation). This note is the suite: what is in it, why,
how it is weighted, and what it costs. Numbers on cost are estimates until
the first baseline.

## Purpose

To tell good changes from neutral ones, across the kinds of music the
compiler meets, quickly. The rules:
- It reports evidence; we decide.
- The headline is the first line of every report, never the sole verdict.
- Taste (variety, how a ride looks) is judged by eye, not scored here.

Difficulty comes from real musical or physical challenges, not from harder
asks added to lower the scores.

## Two kinds of case, reported separately

1. **Automatic arrangements:** the plan chooses the geometry, as in
   production. They test how the compiler uses its capabilities. Their
   categories make **the V7 headline**.
2. **Explicit geometry requests (E):** an ordinary groove where a few
   consecutive gaps ask for one geometry. They test whether a construction
   can be realized. They are reported in their own block: headline, rides
   finished, and requested gaps realized. They are not mixed into the main
   headline.

## Categories (automatic)

Each category weighs equally, specs weigh equally within a category, and
seeds are averaged. The weights are printed in every report.

| category | what it stresses | specs | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| G | ordinary groove | luna, amor, tiki, amour `spec.ts` | existing |
| P1 | short audible pairs at speed | amour, tiki + **amor** | +1 new |
| P2 | voice-led close words | amour, tiki + **amor** | +1 new |
| P3 | calm floats into a slam | amor, tiki + **amour** | +1 new |
| P4 | intense dense sections | amour, luna + **tiki** | +1 new |
| P5 | breaks, drops, re-entries | amor, luna + **amour** | +1 new |
| P6 | long ride (108 s), endings | amour 108 s | existing |
| P7 | **ride-outs:** long riding on geometry at low air, 2–7 s gaps over sustained passages | **one per song** | 4 new |

The intentions are borrowed from V2's low-air endurance ladder and V4's
supported passages, and re-authored on real music against today's contract.

## Explicit requests (E)

Each is a song's `spec.ts`, with three consecutive gaps asking for one
geometry, in three places (early, middle, late). This follows V6's fixed
cases, without V6's repetition: one case per construction.

| case | geometry | rail |
|---|---|---|
| E-fold | fold | offered |
| E-sweep | S-sweep | offered |
| E-ripples | ripples | offered |
| E-terraces | terraces | offered |
| E-scattered | scattered | offered |
| E-handover | arcs with a rail hand-over | required |

Six songs-and-contexts are spread over the four songs.

## Size, seeds and cost

- **Size:** 21 automatic specs (8 categories) plus 6 explicit, 27 in all.
  This is under the ~40 budget on purpose: every spec must earn its place.
- **Seeds:** rounds at 101, 202, 303 and 404 (the `v7` panel); confirmation
  seeds 505, 606, 707 and 808 (`v7-confirm`).
- **Cost, estimated:** one round is 27 rides, about 3–4 minutes for a
  candidate against a cached reference at 44 jobs; four rounds take about
  15 minutes. The 108 s ride is the longest single job (about 4 minutes).
- **Held-out songs** stay their own panel. **Reserve:** the six existing
  `sealed-*` specs, unchanged, used sparingly. Each use wears them out.

## Build steps

1. Harness:
   - the `v7` and `v7-confirm` panels;
   - explicit construction requests in a bench export
     (`construction: [{from, to, family, rail}]`), compiled as the
     automatic plan with those gaps replaced;
   - the E block in the report.
2. Author the new specs. Land only on measured events of each song, and
   explain the reasons in each spec's header, as for the bench specs.
3. First baseline of `main` on `v7`, 4 rounds. Check:
   - every spec compiles;
   - the spread of headlines by category (no category saturated or all
     zeros);
   - the per-seed variation;
   - the E realization rate.

   Adjust specs only at this point, for authoring errors, never to move a
   score. Then freeze V7.0. A later change makes V7.1.
4. Use it: re-judge the overnight branches.

## Not in V7

- Verdicts and probabilities (see the tooling principles).
- V3–V6 catalogs as targets.
- Synthetic (audio-less) timelines.
- Near-duplicate variants.
