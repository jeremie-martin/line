# Re-judging last night's ideas on V7.0 (2026-11-01)

Each run: `npm run compare -- run --reference=main --candidate=<branch> --panel=v7`
(4 rounds, 22 automatic specs plus 6 explicit). Hypotheses are stated before
the runs.

## Hypotheses
- **R0, delete the ending refinement** (`exp/no-tail-refinement`). Last
  night: −0.2 on bench, one sealed long ride lost 66 points.
  - Hypothesis: the V7 headline moves by less than 1 point, with about 5%
    less compute, and any loss concentrates on P6 (the long ride: its ending).
- **S0, no steep-arrival preference at all** (`exp/no-steep-arrival`: the
  `steepArrivalFrom` setting removed). Last night only "everywhere" was
  tested (−12.5). This is the owner's question: is the mechanism needed at
  all, not where its threshold sits?
  - No directional hypothesis: the October adoption suggests a loss on strong
    asks (P3, P4, P6), but the compiler has changed since. Every ruler is
    reported.

## Results (V7.0, 4 rounds, 112 matched pairs each, every ride finished in both arms)

| | V7 headline | by category | specs up / down | explicit (E) | physics per ride |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R0, no ending refinement | −0.2 (733.3 vs 733.5) | all between −0.4 and 0.0 | 0 / 22 | −0.2, 216/216 realized | −0.05M (−1.7%) |
| S0, no steep-arrival preference | −6.1 (727.4 vs 733.5) | G −12.8, P1 −17.4, P2 −11.8, P3 +1.9, P4 −4.4, P5 +4.2, P6 −7.3, P7 −0.9 | 6 / 16 | −9.5, 216/216 realized | +0.02M |

- **R0** matches its hypothesis: under 1 point, small and uniform. Every
  spec loses a little (at most −0.8), and P6 is not singled out on V7. The
  phase is not idle: it buys about 0.2 points everywhere, for 1.7% of the
  physics. The decision stays a trade between simplicity and that small,
  consistent gain. (Compile seconds are not comparable here: the two runs
  shared the machine.)
- **S0:** the preference earns its place on most music, but not
  uniformly. The losses concentrate on Amour, the densest song with the
  strongest asks: its spec, words, pairs, long ride and explicit terraces
  lose 33–68 on single rides. Calm floats (P3) and breaks (P5) gain a
  little. It is a mechanism whose value depends on the music, so a cliff
  at 0.6 is worth questioning, but removing it costs 6 points.
