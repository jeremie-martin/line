# Staged Transition Solver Assay

## Purpose

This is the next study-only step toward a general transition generator. It
tests a continuous capture-to-support construction across short, ordinary, and
long outgoing intervals before any compiler source is changed. It is not a
five-second exception, a legacy fallback, or a promotion proposal.

The preceding causal six-frame phase assay is complete and eligible evidence.
Its motion-facing oracle closed none of the dense fixture's 144 rows. That
falsifies that narrow local stencil; it does not falsify a staged construction
whose support is coupled to an exact capture response.

## Hypothesis

Given an immutable physical prefix, the current contact's authored impact, and
one bounded state-normalized capture control, a static capture can close the
current event. Exact replay then exposes a physical handoff. A continuous,
duration-aware support/release curve beginning at that handoff can change the
outgoing occupancy without changing the protected prefix or reattributing the
capture. The same formulation must work by scale on dense, ordinary, and
3--7-second low-air intervals; it may be unavailable when the necessary axis
is unspecified.

## Causal Boundary

```text
physical prefix + current impact
  -> side-aware capture stencil
  -> exact replay through selected event and response horizon H
  -> sealed physical handoff { point, tangent, flipped }
  -> literal outgoing availability + authored air only
  -> support/release curve
  -> exact composite replay and measurement
```

Capture construction cannot read the outgoing interval, next contact, next
impact, other outgoing axes, fixture identity, source path, seed, score, or a
previous row outcome. The capture stencil is completed before outgoing data is
released and selects the first structurally closed control in a declared
ordering.

The support stage receives only:

- the sealed selected capture handoff and exact response timing;
- outgoing `startFrame`, `endFrame`, interval/sample counts, and authored
  `air` when it exists.

It does not receive the next impact, case or seed identity, score, speed,
amplitude, elevation, or a target-dependent release point. The next contact is
an outcome measurement, never a construction target.

## Physical Form

`staged_capture.ts` will be a fresh study leaf. It may reuse pure curve
arithmetic from historical code, but it must not import the old capture
realizer or closure as an authority. In particular, the historical realizer
hard-codes `flipped: false` and protects only through the incoming interval's
start; neither property is acceptable here.

Each capture control explicitly crosses turn chirality with collision side.
Every capture and support segment keeps the selected physical `flipped` side,
and the capture emits the single handoff `{ point, tangentDeg, flipped }`.
The support begins continuously at that handoff. Extent is derived from exact
post-response air accounting in reference-speed frames, while subdivision is
derived from chord/turn error rather than duration buckets. Release is the
finite end of the support curve; there is no endpoint rail aimed at the next
landing.

## Exact Guards

- The composite static terrain must preserve the full engine trace and have no
  proposed-line collision through `current.endFrame - 2`, the frame before the
  earliest permitted event under the sealed +/-1 ownership convention.
- The selected event must be owned by a capture role within the permitted
  window, survive through its persistence and impact-response windows, and
  have no local off-beat event.
- Adding support must preserve capture identity, capture impact measurement,
  and exact trace through `H`; a support collision before its permitted start
  invalidates the row.
- Every result retains line geometry, role IDs, collision witnesses, exact
  trace identities, observed current event, response handoff, outgoing
  accounting, next-boundary measurement, and elapsed/simulation cost.
- A static positive rail crosses both one-way sides and must admit exactly the
  motion-facing side. Failure makes the full study ineligible.

## Fixed Controls and Panel

The initial capture stencil has 48 controls: the existing 24 compact geometric
controls crossed with both one-way collision sides. Its ordering is fixed and
selection is capture-only. Support is intentionally small: fixed deterministic
scales of the exact air-accounted extent, with a neutral curvature first. A
later 1/2/4/8 proposal-count sensitivity read can determine whether more than a
small bounded menu is necessary; hundreds of controls are a falsifier, not an
implementation plan.

The initial calibration panel is fixed before the study runs:

- dense recovery and independent dense-240ms recovery;
- shifted pickup and an ordinary representative transition;
- one impact-led representative transition;
- the correlated 3/4/5/6/7-second low-air duration ladder;
- an undefined-axis control and a zero-impact control, which remain
  unavailable unless a separately justified neutral-contact formulation exists.

The ladder measures continuity with duration but is one family, not five
independent confirmations. No case-specific control, duration threshold, or
post-hoc winner is permitted.

## Readout and Falsifiers

The assay compares capture-only, support-only where physically meaningful,
combined, and the ordinary baseline under declared exact-simulation budgets.
It reports current closure, protected-prefix integrity, support intrusion,
outgoing air residual, speed residual as a measured diagnostic, next-boundary
survival/ownership, and line/proposal/simulation cost.

The formulation is rejected or revised when any of these occur:

1. the positive side-control fails or the protected prefix drifts;
2. a viable row requires a large oracle or case-specific control selection;
3. capture identity changes when support is added;
4. results work only on the two dense witnesses or only on one duration;
5. ordinary controls show a material validity or quality regression;
6. undefined axes are silently converted into synthetic targets.

## Promotion Gate

A study result is not a headline claim. Only a compact, causal, mixed-panel
form that survives the guards above may be added as a default-off labelled
shadow source. That source must use normal `tryCandidateLines` admission,
remain inert when unavailable, pass fixed-cost ordinary controls, and then
enter the existing V2 Stage-0, family, and D-side workflow. Qualification
tracks remain held out throughout formulation and family selection.
