# Palettes, round 7: notes

**Approach.** I designed each scheme by hand from a named source (a print tradition, a painter, a film grade, a material) and did not derive it from a metric. I then checked lightness afterwards, using CIE L*. The rules:
- the ink sits at least 63 L* from the paper;
- the accent sits about 25 L* or more from the bars;
- the pencil sits 12–27 L* from the paper.

Colour may live in the ink when the ink stays dark (Cahier, Toile) or bright (Kintsugi). I added two new families: mid-tone papers (Kraft, Vegas) and an accent that is *darker* than the bars (Toile). Every candidate was rendered in the lab copy (`mock/`, made by `work/inject.mjs`) at lua 36.5, los 45 and petrunko 28 in the Swatch concept, and I looked at each one. `sheet.png` shows the 10 finalists in three rows, one per moment.

**Killed (6 of 16).**
- Morandi (putty, warm grey, a dusty blue accent): tasteful but the accent did not jump out, and it was a weaker Sumi.
- Chalk (blackboard green-grey): it reads too close to Moss, which the owner rejected, and it is generic.
- Klein night (IKB paper): too saturated over a full screen. Cyanotype is the same idea, gentler.
- Barragán (yellow wall, magenta bars, a purple or white accent): it has the same "aggressive" risk as Riso.
- Pink 'Un (FT salmon, Oxford-blue bars, orange): clear, but corporate and without a soul.
- Vegas2 (a desaturated Vegas): lost its identity and sat halfway to Dune and Kraft.

**Most sure:** Kintsugi (a gold line on black lacquer, unlike anything so far), Cahier, Kraft, Cyanotype, Ravilious.
**Least sure:**
- Vegas: a full-screen orange for 60 s may tire the eye.
- Toile: the dark accent is quiet, and the paper has to stay cream, not pink.
- Kraft, Vegas and Cafe share a structure (dark bars, a light accent). If the owner wants only one mid-tone paper, keep Kraft.

Café and Lavender are refinements of schemes the owner has already seen (Espresso with amber, and a less-pink Mauve).
