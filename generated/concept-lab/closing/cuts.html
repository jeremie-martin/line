Choose the 30 to 45 seconds of each song that become its short video. The band is the song: its loudness, its sections, the proposed cuts above it, and the cut being chosen between two handles you can drag. It plays as the short would loop, end straight into start. “Match” says how well the end is in step with the music that leads into the start (1 is the same rhythm). Keys: Space play, E hear the cut (the last three seconds into the start), S snap the end to the best loop nearby, 1–3 a proposal, Enter save this cut, ↑ ↓ songs, N next song without a cut.
