Say what a ride should follow in each passage of a song, without marking every note. The rows are the notes found in each separated stem (taller is sharper). A song starts with its sections as passages (drag across the rows to make another; a new one cuts into those it crosses). While the music plays the passage you hear is the chosen one: tick the stems to follow in it and move their sliders until the clicks you hear are the notes you want. Or tap along for a bar or two: “Fit to my taps” sets the sliders of the stems you ticked from them (with none ticked, it picks the stem your taps agree with best), and “Keep only the slots I tapped” repeats your bar over the whole passage. A spec's landings can be heard as clicks the same way, before any video exists. Keys: Space play, T tap, B a big hit here (the few that must hit hardest; again to remove), [ ] a passage from here to there, 1–4 follow voice, other, drums, bass, L loop the passage, C next click row, ← → two seconds, Enter save, ↑ ↓ songs.
