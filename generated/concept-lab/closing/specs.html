DecisionsSongsNew specs

The second set of specifications (Codex), song by song, against each song's current spec: whole videos with the same look and zoom lane. Switch version at the same moment, mark the song, and write what you see; a note can hold several times. Keys: V next version, 1 take the new spec, 2 take it after changes, 3 keep the current spec, T write the video's time into the note, ↑ ↓ previous and next song, N next song without a mark, Space play. In the note, Ctrl+Enter saves and Alt+T writes the time. Marks and notes are saved at once.
