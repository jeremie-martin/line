Shakes in quick succession are a matter of the song

A hit shakes when its strength is 0.6 or more. The table counts those hits, and how many the “shakes spaced” option reduces: those that follow, within one second, a shake at least three quarters their size. The reduction is by half when the gap is half a second, and never below a quarter. A hit stronger than the ones before it keeps its shake.
