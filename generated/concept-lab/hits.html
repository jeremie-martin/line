Shakes in quick succession are a matter of the song

A hit shakes when its strength is 0.6 or more. The table counts those hits, and how many of them follow another shaking hit within one second. Those are the ones the “shakes spaced” option reduces: by half when the gap is half a second, down to a quarter at most.
