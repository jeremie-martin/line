The cleanup

  • One source of truth: v1.js holds the settled defaults with the reason for each. Every look and question builds on it. The palettes moved, verbatim and in order, into palettes.js.
  • Nothing undecided was removed. Only truly dead code went: unused helpers, and one redundant address read. Parked and older options stay, and their labels now say whether they’re v1 or only act with the older hit responses.
  • Proof that the cleanup changed nothing: 35 frames across every look, the defaults, older options, palettes and old rides hash identically before and after. The 82 interaction checks pass.
  • The ledger is rewritten as a clear current state: v1 at a glance, every piece, the open items in one list, the library, and how we work. The previous one is kept verbatim in ledger-history/.
  • Rebased on the latest rework/foundations. The full repository check passes.
