What should a hit feel like? A second look

The same four, with plain names, a bigger zoom you can actually see, the slider back, and the curves.

→Four hits, whole songsTomada, Petrunko, Los Voltaje and Circus Minimus, each in A–D. Switch at the same moment, with sound. →The question, with the sliderThe four on the strongest moments, also on Paper and on Black. “Exaggerate the hit effects” multiplies every effect alike (×1 to ×4), to see what you are looking at.

The words

Shake
The whole frame is knocked along the hit’s direction and springs back. Today’s (I called it the “thud” before: same thing).
Zoom
The camera zooms in on the rider for an instant and eases back. The rider stays where it is on screen.
Split
The red and blue channels pull apart for an instant. In all four, unchanged.
Flash
The picture fades toward the paper colour for an instant. Today’s.
Colour boost
What is drawn on the paper (ink, line, rider, band, sun, title) gets more saturated and more contrasted for an instant. The paper itself does not change. (I called it “surge” before.)

The four

  • A · Today, the approved v1: shake, split, flash.
  • B · Zoom: zoom, split, colour boost.
  • C · Zoom, shake on strong hits: B, plus the shake on strong hits only (from strength 0.65).
  • D · No camera motion: split and a stronger colour boost; the camera never moves. Unchanged from round 24.

Every effect follows the hit’s own measured strength, from its strongest frame. Since round 24, the zoom is bigger (20 % closer at strength 0.85 instead of 12 %) and eases back over 0.3 s instead of about 0.1 s, so the eye can follow it.

The curves

How much of each effect a hit gets, by its strength. The shaded band is where half of all hits fall (0.47 to 0.70, on the eight lab songs); the dashed line is the median, 0.59. Point at a chart to read it.
