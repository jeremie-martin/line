Round twenty-six · 8 November

A shake for the strongest hits?

Answered: C, the shake from strength 0.6, with the split and the colour boost (8 November). It is now v1: every look, film and video.

No camera motion is the base now: the clarity was “a complete game changer”, and on Circus it won by a wide margin. The zoom is gone. One question left: should the strongest hits still shake, and from what strength?

→Four versions, whole songsTomada, Circus Minimus, Petrunko and Los Voltaje, each in A–D. Switch at the same moment, with sound.

The words

Shake
The whole frame is knocked along the hit’s direction and springs back.
Split
The red and blue channels pull apart for an instant. In all four, unchanged.
Flash
The picture fades toward the paper colour for an instant. Only in today’s.
Colour boost
What is drawn on the paper (ink, line, rider, band, sun, title) gets more saturated and more contrasted for an instant. The paper itself does not change.

The four

  • A · Today, the approved v1: a shake on every hit, split, flash. You preferred it on the strongest hit.
  • B · No camera motion: split and colour boost; the frame never moves. Round 25’s winner for clarity.
  • C · Shake from 0.6: B, plus a shake on strong hits, rising smoothly from strength 0.6 to today’s 24 px at 0.85 (like the flash’s curve). On the eight lab songs about half the hits shake at all, most only a little: one in eight (above 0.75) by more than 15 px.
  • D · Shake from 0.7: the same, from strength 0.7 to 24 px at 0.9. About one hit in four shakes at all, and only the top 3 % (above 0.8) by more than 12 px.

Was the zoom what we had before? Close: in rounds 6 to 8 a “punch” (the frame closing in) was tried and not chosen either. Twice is enough; it is gone from the code and noted in the ledger.
