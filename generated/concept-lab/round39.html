Which themes we refine, one by one

Each family below says why it is in, the one problem I would address, and how. Move anything in or out, add a word where you disagree, then copy your answers to me.

Four problems several themes share

Each is settled first on the themes named, in the lab, and the answer then carried to the others (you still see each one, as a quick confirmation).

  1. The track is not a colour. Near-white on dark grounds, near-black on light ones. Only Negoro breaks it today: cream instead of orange won by about 1.1. This becomes a rule; no question needed.
  2. The hot bar stands apart from the bars, in hue and not only lightness: a neighbouring hue separates without clashing (Dune: amber, a neighbour, 0.65 and 1.01; vermilion, the same hue, −0.24; cobalt, the opposite side, −0.48). Settled on Dune, Blushing Dawn and Negoro.
  3. On light grounds the band holds its own against the track. A thin band under a heavy track is unbalanced; bold strands beat thin by 0.8 on Black Iris with nothing else changed. Settled on Lavender Dusk and Paper, then Wisteria.
  4. Grounds. Dark grounds: warm only when nearly black (Rothko yes, Madder no); green and blue carry colour well. Every theme clearly light or clearly dark. Settled on Gilt and Amber, Moss Ground, Celadon and Kraft.
