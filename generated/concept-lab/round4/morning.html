How intensity works (after the blind test, please)

Two signals from the ride: how fast the rider is going, and how much impact it takes per second (rate × strength as one sum). Strength earns its place: it varies more through a ride than rate does. Each signal gets a 6-second median, which ignores anything brief in either direction but keeps real changes, then light smoothing, and a shared scale for all songs so a flat song stays flat. The two are averaged, with a hard ceiling on change. Because the whole ride is known, there’s no lag: changes centre on the moment they happen.
