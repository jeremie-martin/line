Timing: your ear was right too

I lined up the sound around every strong hit, on eight songs, and averaged it, so no detector is involved:

MomentRelative to the rider’s first touch
The sound’s rise (half-way up)−6 to +10 ms, every song
“Later”, the measured hit time+25 ms, one physics frame
The impact’s peak+25 ms typical, +50 ms or later on about 30 % of hits

So the first touch already lands with the sound, and “Later” really was about 25 ms late. Accents stay on the touch, which is measured per hit, with no fixed delay in the lab. The touch is quantised to 25 ms physics frames, and these averages say nothing about each single hit.

The jolt is a different thing: a −15 ms shift built into the compiler, so it moves the tracks themselves. The code records it as your own choice by ear in June, from 0, −15 and −25 ms on one song. It seems to be working, since touches land within about 10 ms of the sound. I’d rather replace it than just delete it: removing it alone would probably move touches about 15 ms early. The clean version aims each contact at that hit’s measured sound, with no constant. That’s a compiler change with its own paired comparison, outside the lab, so tell me if you want it opened. My first detector misread Los Voltaje by about 60 ms; only the direct averaging above is reliable.
