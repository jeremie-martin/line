What I found about today’s hits

I read the shipped recipe instead of guessing, and it isn’t what either of us thought. Today’s effects are not limited to the top 10 % of hits:

  • Almost every hit counts. Each hit above a low threshold adds to a level that decays quickly and is capped. An ordinary hit already reaches full force, so strength barely shows, and dense passages pile up into one long shake.
  • The shake wobbles: 10 px on two axes at 9 Hz, plus a slight rotation, whatever the blow’s direction.
  • The split is 14 px, sideways, over the whole picture. The flash whitens the screen, up to 30 %.

Held was the opposite: each hit alone, a single push, with a split and flash two to three times smaller on ordinary hits. That gap is probably the “oh yeah” you were missing.

So the hits are now built from separate parts, and questions 1–4 take them one at a time:

  1. the shake
  2. the split
  3. the flash
  4. the logic that drives them

Everything not being asked about stays at today’s, so your reference never moves.

The other two

  • Height: Follows direction is kept, as you chose. Only the rider’s height varies (38, 40 or 43 % of the screen), and your two Los Voltaje moments are there, on the old ride where you saw them.
  • Palettes: ten new ones, designed from scratch by hand, each from a real source (an exercise book, Toile de Jouy, Ravilious, kraft screenprint, Blade Runner 2049, cyanotype, kintsugi, Rothko…). They come from no measure, and the lines are no longer all near-black. Hokusai is there to compare against. No sun, since it’s a separate element.
