You were right about the split

The split in the hit’s direction did show more, and two things explain it:

  • Direction. A split shows fully across a line and hides along it. Today’s goes sideways, mostly along the lines; the hit’s direction mostly crosses them.
  • My lab copy wasn’t faithful. In production the split touches only the ride; the band is a separate layer on top. My lab split the band too, where today’s sideways split lit up every vertical bar. It now splits the ride only, as production does.

A flash, but rare

You said most of the time there should be none. The gate opens on each hit’s own strength, from 0.82 to full at 0.94, never by ranking. That gives about 1 to 10 flashes a minute depending on the song, and none on most hits. You can try it on Dune, Café and Hokusai: the moment now stays while you switch options, which fixes the bug you hit.

Backgrounds

You said the bars and lines were great but the backgrounds weren’t, and that it’s hard to put a finger on. So each paper question keeps a palette’s line, bars and accent exactly, and moves only the paper in plain directions: less colour, almost neutral, lighter, darker. Your picks across the three should show what your eye wants, more than any measure could.
