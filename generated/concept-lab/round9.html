Round nine · 7 November

Voting fixed, and themes as a whole

Six questions in the lab. The lab itself has been through a full check, so voting should finally just work.

→Six questionsThe split (asked again, now selectable), how often the flash fires, the shake with an exaggerate slider, then Kraft, Cyanotype and Rothko as whole themes.

Why you couldn’t vote, and what changed

A multi-choice vote was saved as one text joined with commas, then split on commas when read back. So any option whose name contains a comma, like “Toward the paper, hardest only”, fell apart and could never be selected. That had been there for four rounds.

Then, as you suggested, a separate agent audited every interaction in the lab and fixed 18 more problems:

  • a swipe or an arrow key dropped “On Dune” while the label still claimed it;
  • Next, while a song was loading, could show the previous question’s option;
  • answer buttons were too small for a thumb;
  • the “All rounds” link covered the picture’s corner.

An automated suite now taps through everything like a phone (82 checks) and must pass before a round goes out. The picture never changed: the same frames are byte-identical before and after.

Your questions

  • Did the split use to touch the band and title? Yes. Until round 8 my lab split everything. Production only ever splits the ride, so I matched it. If you liked the band fringing, it can come back as an option.
  • Is the flash fixed, or does it follow strength? It follows strength twice: the gate decides which hits flash at all, and inside it a stronger hit flashes brighter and longer. Question 2 tries three gates: about 1–11, 11–18 or 20–28 flashes a minute.
  • Halfway had red at the bottom. You were right; it flipped the hit’s direction. Red now stays on the hit’s side.
  • Shake: a temporary slider multiplies it up to ×4 so you can see the differences. It is only a viewing aid; it resets on the next question and never reaches films.

Themes: a pattern in your picks

In every scheme you love, the bars carry more colour than the paper: Hokusai’s paper has about a quarter of its bars’ colour, Café’s and Dune’s under half. Cyanotype as designed inverts it, with the paper more colourful than the bars, and its “less colour” paper, which you preferred by far, restores it. Kraft inverts it too.

So, as you said, these are whole themes, not papers:

  • Kraft’s darker paper with richer bars, and the lighter paper with lighter bars and a dark accent;
  • Cyanotype with a little more colour, integrated like “less colour”;
  • Rothko’s original paper with bars moved toward dusty rose or peach.

It’s a hypothesis your picks will confirm or break, not a rule.
