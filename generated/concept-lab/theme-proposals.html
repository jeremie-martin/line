Theme proposals · 8 November

Three fixes and five new themes

From a design study of the twelve themes against everything you have said about colour. Nothing here is applied yet: all real renders of Circus Minimus with today’s v1, at 18 s, inside the drop on a hit (the colour boost showing), and at 60 s.

Three fixes

  • Aubergine’s drop: its night partner has carmine bars, the same colour as the accent it receives, so the loudest bars vanish for the whole drop. Proposed: lavender bars from the same Wada combination, or, quieter, dark aconite bars.
  • Dune to Espresso: the amber accent is weak on Espresso’s latte bars. Proposed: Café as the drop partner, built for Dune’s amber.
  • Black Iris: the Iris band is drawn in fixed navy, nearly invisible on black paper. Proposed: draw it lit whenever the paper is dark, as in Night (a two-line change in the renderer).

Five new themes

  • Seashell Night (Wada 194): shell-pink paper, steel-blue bars, Night at the drop.
  • Peony to Magenta (Wada 204 → 144): pale blue paper, magenta bars; at the drop the paper goes black, the bars stay.
  • Kraft Poster: kraft paper, deep rust bars, the title, all ink. The set’s only mid-tone paper.
  • Cyanotype: blueprint-blue paper, brighter periwinkle bars, a yellow sun, all ink.
  • Apricot (Wada 253): near-black paper, apricot bars, all ink.

Tell me which to keep; the kept ones join the themes and the showcase.
