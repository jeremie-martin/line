## Owner requirements

Use only normal type-0 lines and coherent, visible physical arc primitives.
Point-control constellations or isolated controls disguised with decorative
curves do not satisfy the owner’s requirement. Arc shape, geometry, search,
planning, feedback and architecture may evolve. Single arcs and partial or
full paired guidance should emerge from geometry and measured physical need;
there is no motif quota or prescribed behavior frequency.

Benchmark V2, scorer, authored targets, catalog, validity, physics and actual-frame
accounting remain fixed. Research compute is unrestricted. Accepted compilers
must earn their measured results inside the execution allowance. Earlier design
choices, workflow closures, study caps and deferrals are historical evidence,
not research restrictions. This milestone does not establish a ceiling.
