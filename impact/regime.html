What you get — delivered redirArc (px)

x: authored impact · y: achieved redirArc p50 (band = p25–p75). Faint horizontal lines = each regime's SOFT/VSTRONG anchors.

What it scores — felt impact (normImpact)

x: authored impact · y: scored impact under the regime's anchors. Dashed = ideal y=x.

Reading guide: a good regime's right-panel curve is monotone and hugs the diagonal across the whole range — every authored value lands distinct and near its felt meaning. A dead floor (flat at 0 on the left of the curve) means soft authoring is unreachable; a saturated/short ceiling (curve tops out well below 1 on the right) means strong authoring can't be expressed. The left panel shows whether that's a geometry limit (px curve itself flat/short) or a calibration one (px fine but anchors map it wrong).
