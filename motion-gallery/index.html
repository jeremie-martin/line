What this comparison measures

The adherence score uses the unchanged V4 scoring functions on these research passages. It is not a V4 headline or a measure of visual quality. A score of zero means the physical or timing contract failed; the reason remains visible.

These are recorded physics views at 40 frames per second, without music or production post-processing. The search allowance is a ceiling, not work that must be spent. This study compares whole tracks; it does not yet measure mixing styles or restricting how often scattered segments may be used.
