Requested vs achieved impact

Mean achieved strength (line.strike.v3) for each band of requested strength, authored specs. Unmatched beats contribute zero. The diagonal is a perfect match. The shaded band runs from about 0.06 (the gentlest sampled touchdown) to 0.9 (the sampled 45° transition); an isolated near-vertical drop can go higher.

What the strength scale means

Single hits on a flat floor at rising speed into the surface, measured with the product's impact measure. Survival depends on how the rider meets the floor: at 45° the rider crashes above about 0.9, but a near-vertical drop onto flat ground survives even a maximal hit (about 9 px/frame into the floor). The current compiler delivers about 0.6–0.7 in the densest passages. This observed range is not a proved physical ceiling.
