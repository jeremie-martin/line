Motion review

41 short stretches from the handcrafted videos, each about two to four seconds, played in a loop with the music.

The part to judge is where the rider is on the track between two asked hits, not the jump before or after. It is highlighted under the video, and the video gets a yellow frame while it plays. The question is the same every time: in that part, does the rider move in ways the music does not call for?

Answer with the keys 1 to 4. Click or drag on the bar to move in the clip; the arrow keys step back and forward. The tags and the note are optional. Your answers are saved as you go, and you can stop and come back. It takes about ten minutes.
